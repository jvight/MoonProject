# Player-experience lens (owner's rule, 2026-10-13)

The owner: "the most important skill for game design is asking what draws players in, what keeps them to the end,
what feeling the game gives, and why Subnautica worked so well — and setting development goals from that."

Read `docs/design/north-star.md` first (the living analysis). Then, for the thing you're designing, answer every
question below **in the doc itself, before the mechanics**. If an answer is weak, fix the design, not the answer.

## 1. The five questions
1. **Hook:** what makes a player *want* this in the first seconds they see it? What do they see or hear before they
   understand it? (Subnautica: an alien ocean you can't see the bottom of.)
2. **Pull:** what question does it put in the player's head that they need answered? Where is the next "I wonder
   what's over there"? (Subnautica: the Aurora on the horizon, the signal, the dark below.)
3. **Hold:** why do they come back tomorrow and the next hour? What grows (07, the map, the base, the knowledge), and
   is that growth *visible*?
4. **Finish:** why do they play to the end? What answer or payoff is promised from early on, and is it kept?
5. **Feeling:** name the feeling in one or two words (e.g. "lonely wonder", "quiet pride") and say exactly which
   sights, sounds, timings and beats produce it. It must fit VISION's pillars (solitude, calm, warmth).

## 2. Then check the design against these
- **Learn, then improve** (memory learn-then-improve): name the Subnautica mechanic you're borrowing, *why* it works
  there, and what Lunarion does better or differently. Never copy without the reason.
- **Logic** (memory living-moon, story-logic): does every element have a believable reason to exist and behave as
  it does?
- **Purpose reads** (memory purpose-must-read): would a new player understand what this is for without text?
- **Visible progression** (memory visible-progression-priority): does the player see their growth on 07 or home?
- **Calm, never stressful:** tension comes from range, darkness and the unknown, never from threat or punishment.

## 3. Close with a playtest signal
State how we'll know it works: one observable behaviour in a play-through, e.g. "within 10 minutes the player drives
toward the rim without being told to", "the player stops at 06 for more than 3 s". Captures and the owner's
play-through check these.
