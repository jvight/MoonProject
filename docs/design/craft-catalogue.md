# Craft catalogue (1.0): everything 07 can build, fit, repair and make its own

> Author: the game design session, for the Director and the owner. Design only.
>
> The owner: "Isn't that too few things 07 can craft and build? Like WALL-E, for example." The answer: **2 kit
> sockets limit what 07 carries at once, not what it can build.** The catalogue is wide; the loadout is a choice.
>
> Every entry passes `blueprint-fit.md`:
> - standard: **A** ILC-R built directly · **B** SSR kit fitted when docked · **C** foreign, needs the adapter ·
>   **D** never;
> - assembly without hands;
> - interfaces at 0.35–1.46 m;
> - build energy ≤ 1 cell at its first tier;
> - a job on a robot base.
>
> Built on: the owner-approved mounts-vs-sockets model; `opening-v2.md` (the opening's 4 builds, §10's start kit);
> `wear-and-reclaimers.md` (the service gantry, repair kit); `inventions.md`; `0.6-world-regions.md`;
> `0.6-living-moon.md`; M3-25, M3-26, M3-27.
>
> **Cost shorthand:** M Metal · W Wiring · O Optics · Al Alloy · G Glass · Ox Oxygen (blueprint-fit gap: to add) ·
> C Coolant · Cr Crystal · Ch Circuit Chips · S Superconductor. **⚡** = build energy in cells.
>
> **Phase:** O = the opening (first 45 min) · 0.5 = the rest of the basin · 0.6 = the Long Mare · 0.7 = the Rim
> Terraces · 0.8 = the Shadowed Crater and lava tube · 1.0 = the Peak.

## 0. Player experience (skill `player-experience`)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | The first build (the pad) rises out of 07's own hatch-pressed members and welds in front of you. *I* made that. |
| **Pull** | Every region shows a new standard of thing to build: a rail across the mare, a bridge over a rift, a mirror that throws sunlight into the dark. **Something new to build every 6–8 minutes** (§3). |
| **Hold** | The base becomes recognisably *07's*: its brick ramps, its lit cable runs, its sun-tracking solar flowers, its cairns on the horizon. The loadout of two sockets changes per trip ("winch and drums for the mare; thrusters and drill for the crater"). |
| **Finish** | The great dish is the last and largest build. Everything before it was practice for it. |
| **Feeling** | **Quiet craft and pride:** a small machine making a place, one weld at a time. |

**Learn, then improve:**
- **WALL-E** compacts trash into cubes and stacks them into towers: expressive, repetitive, *his*.
- **Subnautica** gives breadth (bases, vehicles, modules) but much of it is storage busywork.
- **Ours:** expressive builds that each **do a job** (bricks become ramps and causeways; cable runs carry power and
  light the pulse; solar flowers charge on the route). Breadth comes from regions, not from duplicate lockers.

## 2. Expressive builds (what WALL-E's cubes are to WALL-E)
Each one has a real job, and each one makes the base visibly the *player's*:
- **The brick press and its blocks** (17, 23, 27, 31): scrap and regolith become interlocking blocks. The player
  places them as ramps, causeways, cairns and the sunshade garage. **The player's architecture on the moon,** with a
  rhythmic, satisfying stamping loop.
- **Cable runs** (21): drawn by driving. The network pulse runs along *the player's own lines* at night.
- **Solar flowers** (5): a sun-tracking field that turns together with the slow polar Sun.
- **Cairn beacons** (27): the player's own landmarks, seen from far and on the chart.
- **Lamp posts** (26): Mule's unfinished job, finished by the player, lighting their routes.
- **The paint and stencil booth** (32): the player's number and colours on what they build.

## 4. What makes building feel good *for 07* (no hands)
1. **The hatch press:** stock goes in through the rear hatch, and the hold's former stamps members out with a felt
   *thunk-thunk* in rhythm (D pentatonic). It is the start of every build.
2. **Self-raising frames:** members slide out and lock; masts telescope; arrays unfold. The structure *stands
   itself up*; 07 never lifts.
3. **The weld beam:** welds run as **2–3 short seams**, like the cutter's segments (M3-26). Each seam is a short
   victory with a bead that cools from white to dark.
4. **Laying by driving:** the cable reel and the block placer turn driving into building. The player *draws* a power
   line or a causeway by moving.
5. **The first power-on:** every powered build ends with lamps, a hum and 07's annunciator "done" tone (A). Linked
   builds pass the **network pulse** to their neighbours.
6. **The cell cost is felt:** 07's battery lamps dim a step as it welds (opening v2 §10). Building is work, and the
   pad that recharges it afterwards is the reward.
7. **07's body language** (07-identity, option A, the scheduler's weights): a small settle and a head turn to inspect
   each finished seam, then a look back at the whole structure. Never a cartoon celebration.

## Tóm tắt (Tiếng Việt)
- **Ý chính:** 2 ổ kit chỉ giới hạn thứ 07 *mang theo cùng lúc*, không giới hạn thứ 07 *dựng được*. Danh mục 1.0
  rộng, còn chọn bộ kit cho từng chuyến là một lựa chọn thú vị.
- **Số lượng đến 1.0:** 32 công trình, 4 mục tiêu sửa, 12 kit, 22 cấp thiết bị, 6 xe và máy giúp việc. Tổng cộng
  **72 thứ để làm**; 6 trong đó là công trình "mang dấu ấn" (★).
- **Mỗi mục đều qua quy tắc blueprint-fit:**
  - chuẩn A, B hoặc C;
  - lắp không cần tay;
  - mọi điểm tương tác ở 0,35–1,46 m;
  - dựng tốn tối đa 1 ô pin;
  - có việc thật cho căn cứ robot.
- **Công trình mang dấu ấn** (như những khối rác của WALL-E, nhưng hợp với 07 và có việc thật):
  - máy ép gạch: ép phế liệu và đất thành khối lắp ghép, dùng làm dốc, đường đắp, ụ mốc, nhà che;
  - đường cáp: rải bằng cách lái xe, ban đêm nhịp mạng chạy dọc theo đường người chơi đã vẽ;
  - hoa pin mặt trời: quay theo Mặt Trời, là điểm sạc nhỏ dọc đường;
  - ụ mốc có đèn;
  - cột đèn: làm nốt việc dang dở của Mule;
  - buồng sơn: số hiệu và màu của người chơi trên công trình.
- **Xe và máy giúp việc** (chỉ là máy theo nhiệm vụ, không phải bạn đồng hành):
  - xe trượt chở hàng;
  - xe ray;
  - drone khảo sát;
  - drone sửa chữa: kéo 07 về khi hỏng, thay vai trò của Tilly đã bị bỏ;
  - rô-bốt quét bụi (sửa lại);
  - xe cẩu (sửa lại).
- **Nhịp tiến bộ:** 8–10 bản vẽ mới mỗi giờ, cứ 6–8 phút lại có thứ mới để dựng, không bao giờ quá 15 phút không có
  gì mới.

- **Cảm giác xây dựng của 07:**
