# 0.6+: a world of regions (draft for the owner)

> Author: the game design session, for the Director's review and the owner's decision. Implementation starts after
> 0.5.5 ships.
>
> The owner: staying inside the crater basin is boring. They want a Subnautica-like world of **regions**, a strong
> pull to go farther, and upgrades that unlock range and access.
>
> Builds on:
> - GDD v3 §5 (regions) and §3.7 (range by version);
> - M3-22 (build pads, pylons), M3-27 (materials by region);
> - the 0.5.5 spec (scanning, 07's equipment sockets, derelicts);
> - the level review (the basin's layout).
>
> Memories: `learn-then-improve`, `derelict-variety`, `feel-priority-solitude`, `phased-releases`,
> `visible-progression-priority`.

## 0. Player experience (skill `player-experience`; see `north-star.md`)
| Question | The regions' answer |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | From the basin's west rim the player sees what the basin never had: a vast dark plain to the horizon, a pale **swirl** on it, and an **amber blink** far off (the mare dish). It's the first time the world is visibly bigger than home. |
| **Pull** | Every region shows the next one before it can be reached: from the mare, Meridian's white strobes on the terraces, the crater's green glow, the skylight's shafts. Each also holds a piece of the survey (story v3): *what did they find, and why did nobody come back?* |
| **Hold** | The frontier moves visibly: Cell Pack III and pylons push range, each lit mast turns a lonely place into home (music returns, the chart inks, machines wake), and 07 grows a part per region. |
| **Finish** | The regions are the road to the Peak: each one lights a step of the recovery panel and adds a survey piece, until the uplink sends the proof home. |
| **Feeling** | **Vast, lonely, then less alone.** The mare is the emptiest place in the game (the widest soft room tone, static radio, Earth huge and low over a black plain), until the dish lights and the music and machines come back. |

**Playtest signal:** within 10 minutes of 0.6 content the player drives toward the west rim unprompted, and after
lighting the mare dish they say where they want to go next.

## 1. Learn from Subnautica, then improve
| Subnautica | Its pain | Lunarion's rule |
|---|---|---|
| Biomes you see only once you're inside them, at depth | discovery by accident; a wiki to know where to go | **R1. Every region is seen before it is reached.** A landmark you can see from the previous region (a dish's blink, a bright swirl on dark ground, a skylight's glow) is the lure. |
| Each biome has signature resources | some resources hide in many biomes, so trips blur | **R2. One signature resource per region,** found nowhere else (M3-27), and its look matches the region. |
| Depth gates (pressure, oxygen) | an invisible stat ("hull crush depth") | **R3. Every gate is a real lunar condition you can see:** range, permanent dark, slope, a rille or rift, a skylight drop. Each is opened by **a part fitted in one of 07's sockets** (0.5.5 §3.5), so the gate is visible on 07. |
| Bases you build anywhere | clutter; base-building as a chore | **R4. Forward bases are masts and pylons on predefined pads** (M3-22): a charger, a hop point and a lit chart. They are never a free-build sprawl. |
| Blueprint fragments in the next biome | hunting for fragments in the place you can't yet survive | **R5. The next region's fragments sit at the edge of the current one.** You learn the key at the door. |
| A radio that sends you places | text messages, quest-log energy | **R6. Bell's signals and the friends pull you on.** A pillar, a chirp, a letter, never a marker. |
| A story spine (the Sunbeam, Alien research) | it reveals by reading | **R7. The arc ends at the dish: Power → Link → Dish → Answer.** Each region lights one more step on the lab's recovery panel, which you see. |

**Lunarion's own twist: solitude grows with distance.**
- Every region is lonelier than the last until a mast lights it. The radio thins to static, then near-silence (VISION
  pillar 6).
- **Lighting a region's mast is the moment it becomes "home too":** music returns, the chart inks, a charger glows.
- The player's reward for going far is turning a lonely place into a lit one.

### 2.1 Regions open outward by adjacency (owner, 2026-10-13, memory `world-regions`)
The next region is always one **right next to a region already opened**, so the world grows outward like a stain of
light, never by jumping. The order on the map, with each region's opened neighbour:

| Order | Region | Opens from (adjacent) | Seam |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | Home basin (+ Whispering Canyon) | — (the canyon opens off the basin's east rim) | the chasm |
| **0.6** | **The Long Mare** | the basin | the rille pass through the west rim |
| 0.7 | Rim Terraces | the mare's north shore | the mare–highland boundary |
| 0.8 | Shadowed Crater | the mare's south-west | the crater rim |
| 0.8 | Lava tube | inside the mare | the skylight |
| 1.0 | The Peak road | the terraces' top end | the upper switchback |

No region is reachable across an unopened one. Every lure (§3) is seen from a neighbour that is already open.

## 3. The regions
### 3.0 The rule for each region
**Identity · gate (+ the part) · signature · lure · derelicts · friend or story · masts and pylons · feel (look,
light, colour, sound, driving).**

### 3.1 The Long Mare (Biển Dài): **the recommended 0.6**
- **Identity:** the moon at its emptiest and most beautiful. A flat, near-black basalt plain to the horizon, with Earth
  huge and low over it. The loneliest place in the game until it's lit.
- **Gate: range.** The rille pass is drivable from 0.5, but the mare is ~1 km of open ground with no chargers.
  - The part is **Cell Pack III** (Back socket: the battery spine grows a second module; 6 → 8 cells).
  - It is paired with the **Field Charge Pylon** (M3-22), built on the mare's first pad, which turns the frontier
    into a chain.
  - The gate is honest: you *can* drive in, and the battery shows you why you turn back (ruling 15's calm crawl
    applies).
- **Signature: Ilmenite**, drilled from dark outcrops (the Core Drill, Cutter socket's neighbour on the nose; M3-27).
  It is real geology: mare basalts are titanium-rich. Smelted at home into **Alloy**.
- **Lures:**
  - from the basin's west rim at dusk, a **bright lunar swirl** (a pale sinuous pattern on the dark plain, like
    real ones) and the **slow amber blink of an old comms dish** on the far side;
  - from inside the mare, the **skylight's faint glow** (the lava tube, 0.8) and **the terraces' pale steps**
    (0.7) on the north shore.
- **Derelicts** (mixed kinds):
  - **the stopped convoy:** three haul bots in a line, a load still strapped. Salvage, and the **Cutter Ribs**
    fragments.
  - **the comms dish:** the crew's long-range relay. It is the mast site, and its archive holds 20 years of Ro's
    letters (the radio story).
  - a crashed survey drone.
  - **at the north shore (R5):** a construction bot slid down the slope, holding the **Magnetic Treads**
    fragments: the next region's key at its door.
- **Friend / story:** no friend lives here. It is the place where you're most alone. **Bell's signal** points at the
  dish, and lighting it plays Ro's first unheard letter. The recovery panel's **Link** row gets its first lamp.
- **Masts and pylons:** the comms dish becomes the mare's **mast** (mid-mare). There are **2 pylon pads** (the rille
  mouth and the convoy). Chargers end up ≤ 300 m apart (the 0.5 rule).
- **Feel:**
  - **look:** black-blue basalt with a faint sheen; pale ejecta rays and the swirl as giant wayfinding lines;
  - **light:** a low sun, mile-long shadows from small rocks;
  - **colour:** the darkest palette so far, with warm points rare and precious;
  - **sound:** the widest, softest room tone, the radio only static until the dish is lit;
  - **driving:** **fast and flat**, long straights, the Boost Coils' joy, gentle swells you float over.

### 3.2 Rim Terraces (Bậc thềm vành), 0.7
- **Identity:** high, bright, Earth-facing. You look *down* on home for the first time.
- **Gate: slope.** **Magnetic Treads** (Wheels socket: six matched treaded wheels, the odd wheel healed). They climb
  the marked terrace walls only (DESIGN's lane rule).
- **Signature: Anorthite**, the pale highland rock. Refined into **Aluminium** for light frames: pylons, the Cargo
  Pod, Long Leap's vanes.
  - This is an **M3-27 amendment** (9 raw, 6 refined, inside the Director's cap of ≤ 10 + ≤ 6). Ilmenite moves from
    the terraces to the mare, which is truer to lunar geology.
- **Lure:** seen from the mare's north shore and from the basin: the pale steps, and **Atlas's work lamp** high on a
  ledge.
- **Derelicts:** the crew's **upper station ruins** (station debris), Atlas's cargo cradle, and a fallen dish array.
  **Long Leap** fragments sit at the terraces' top (R5, toward the Peak).
- **Friend:** **Atlas**, the big gentle hauler.
- **Masts and pylons:** a terrace mast and 2 ledge pylon pads.
- **Feel:**
  - **look:** pale gold-lit steps;
  - **light:** the brightest region, with sharp shadows;
  - **sound:** high, still air (no wind: vacuum), metallic ticks of cooling rock;
  - **driving:** climbing, switchbacks, the treads' grip.

### 3.3 Shadowed Crater (Hố bóng tối), 0.8
- **Identity:** eternal night. Frost glitters in 07's lamp. The one place the sun never reaches.
- **Gates: permanent dark and cold.**
  - The part is **Floodlights** (Lamp socket: the lamp bar becomes twin floods).
  - The cold is a gentle **range** pressure: battery use ×1.25 in permanent shadow, eased by a pylon on the rim.
    There is no new damage stat.
  - **The Cell Heater** (owner decision, Q3) cancels the cold drain: a warm amber band round the battery spine,
    glowing softly in the dark (pillar 2: warmth in the cold). It fits a new **Core socket** at the body's centre
    (0.5.5 §3.5's socket list grows by one in 0.8). Its fragments sit at the crater's rim beside Theo's sled.
- **Signature: Water Ice** → Coolant (the Ice Still) and Crystal (Moss's biodome).
- **Lure:** from the mare's south-west, a black bowl with **a faint green glow** inside (Moss's light).
- **Derelicts:** Theo's garden-survey sled; ice-drill equipment; a frozen drone. **Anchor Line** fragments sit at the
  rim (for the lava tube and the Peak).
- **Friend:** **Moss**, the garden bot.
- **Masts and pylons:** a rim mast and 1 floor pylon pad.
- **Feel:**
  - **look and light:** dark, lit only by 07's floods and Moss's glow;
  - **colour:** the biolum palette (#3CFFC2) as magic;
  - **sound:** crystalline ticks, a deep hush;
  - **driving:** slow and careful inside a lit circle.

### 3.4 The lava tube (Ống dung nham), 0.8 or 0.9
- **Identity:** a vaulted cave under the mare. Shafts of sunlight fall through skylights, and crystals sit in the
  walls. Awe, not fear.
- **Gate: the drop.** A 25 m skylight. The **Anchor Line** (Tether socket tier 2: a winch drum) lowers 07 down, and
  a ramp exit leads out (ruling 10's one-way rule).
- **Signature: Rare Earths** (KREEP-rich lava), for Superconductor.
- **Lure:** the skylight's glow on the mare, visible from 0.6 on.
- **Derelicts:** the crew's cave survey station (they meant to shelter here from the storm: a story beat); a
  collapsed rail line.
- **Story:** the crew's storm shelter, never reached. A crew log explains why they left.
- **Mast:** a skylight mast (its signal goes up the shaft).
- **Feel:**
  - **look and light:** columns of light, echo, smooth basalt floors;
  - **sound:** **the only reverb in the game**;
  - **driving:** a tunnel, gentle curves.

### 3.5 The Peak road (Đường lên Đỉnh), 1.0
- **Identity:** the climb you've seen since the first frame.
- **Gates:** rifts (**Long Leap**, Belly socket tier 2), and a rockfall (**Atlas** lifts it).
- **Signature:** the dish station's sealed vaults hold the **last Circuit Chips** (Thermal Cutter), and Rare Earths
  meet them in the Smelter II for Superconductor.
- **Lure:** the red beacon, always.
- **Friend / story:** **the Dish**, and the Answer.
- **Masts:** the Peak's foot mast and 2 road-camp pylons.
- **Feel:** switchbacks, the whole world below, Earth at eye level.

## 5. Recommended 0.6 scope: the Long Mare, done deeply
**Why the mare first** (a change from GDD v3, which put the Terraces in 0.6):
- **It is the strongest contrast to the basin:** dark, flat, fast, the loneliest, Earth huge. That answers "the basin
  is boring" immediately.
- **Its gate (range) uses systems already built:** the battery, chargers, crawl and pylons. New traversal tech (the
  treads' climbing) is riskier and lands in 0.7.
- **Its terrain is cheap to generate and fast to drive:** a big new space without heavy new physics.
- **It shows three future regions from inside it** (the terraces, the crater's glow, the skylight), so 0.6 sets up
  the whole world's pull.

**In scope:**
- the rille pass (streamed terrain boundary);
- the mare terrain with ejecta rays and the swirl;
- the comms dish mast with its letter archive;
- 2 pylon pads;
- the stopped convoy;
- 1–2 more derelicts;
- Ilmenite outcrops plus the Core Drill;
- the Smelter (home pad) for Alloy;
- **Cell Pack III** and **Cutter Ribs** (fragments at the convoy);
- Cell Pack III's fragments at the basin's rille mouth;
- the Magnetic Treads fragments at the north shore (a lure part to 0.7, scannable but not buildable until its sheet
  is complete in 0.7: **decided, a teaser only**, §6 Q6);
- the mare's own sound bed and driving tuning.

**Out of 0.6:** the terraces, crater, tube and Peak (seen, not reached); Atlas and Moss.

**World tech:** today's world is one generated basin. Regions need **terrain chunks streamed around 07**, with the
pass as the boundary. Never a loading screen (VISION: no hard cuts). This is the first engineering decision for the
Director and World.

## 8. Proposed VISION edits (for the Director, who owns VISION)
**1. Pillar 6, "Value, not just hue"**: replace the sentence "A low sun throws long shadows that give the ground
rhythm." with:

> A low sun throws long shadows that give the ground rhythm. Lumen Station sits near the Moon's pole: the Sun skims
> the horizon and circles the sky once per lunar day (2 hours of play), so shadows are always long and slowly turn.
> High ground is nearly always sunlit, the deepest craters never are, and Earth hangs fixed and low, its cool
> earthshine lighting the world but never charging anything.

**2. World concept**, after the paragraph about Earth, add:

> **Light is physical.** Sunlight (warm-white, low) charges solar panels and 07's wing; earthshine (cool, from
> Earth's fixed place) only lights. Places pass between a sunlit phase and an earthlit phase as the Sun circles; the
> earthlit phase is the lavender night the game is known for. The terminator, where the two meet, is where dust
> glows.

**3. The palette table**, note under it:

> Swatches are authored for the earthlit phase. The sunlit phase warms highlights and rim light (it never changes
> the swatches themselves), and permanently shadowed places never get it.
