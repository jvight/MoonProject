# A living moon (0.6+): machine ecology, phenomena, rare life and the databank

> Author: the game design session. Draft for the Director and the owner.
> The owner (2026-10-13, memory `living-moon`): 07 alone driving, mining, crafting and building is boring. It lacks
> the urge and the fun along the way that Subnautica's ecosystem gives. The owner chose **all four layers**.
>
> Sibling of `0.6-world-regions.md` (regions open outward by adjacency, §2.1 there). It builds on:
> - the 0.5.5 spec: scanning (§1), derelicts (§1.7), the guidance budget (§4);
> - feel-pass §7 (07's chirps);
> - M3-25 (Tilly fetches), ruling 15 (the battery).
>
> **Solitude stays the bar** (pillar 6, memory `feel-priority-solitude`). The moon is still mostly silent. Life is
> met, not swarming.

## 0. Rules for everything alive or moving
1. **Never hostile, never horror.** Nothing chases, harms, steals, blocks or startles. A machine in 07's way steps
   aside. Nothing can trap 07 (ruling 7).
2. **Sparse.** At most **one group** of machines in view. A new species encounter about every 3–5 min of driving in
   an open region, and fewer at home. Phenomena are rarer still.
3. **Readable by behaviour, not text.** Each species has a habitat, a behaviour loop and one reaction to 07: shy,
   curious or indifferent. The player learns it by watching.
4. **Every encounter is worth something:** a databank page, a pointer to a find, a path, a helper. Never only
   decoration, never a chore.
5. **Systemic inputs only:** light (sunlit or shadow, 07's lamp), stillness (07 stopped), listening (07's rest ping),
   and distance. No scripted cutscenes for the ecology.
6. **Scanning life never removes it** (the 0.5.5 D2 vanish applies to blueprint fragments only). Scanning a machine,
   a phenomenon or a place draws its lines into the lens and leaves it as it was.

## 1. Machine ecology (the fauna)
Abandoned machines still doing their old jobs, decades after anyone told them to.

**0.6 builds** the mites, the sweeper, the flock and the trencher. That is four species, one per kind of reaction,
the shy one twice.

**How it reads on screen:**
- Machines are small and low-poly in the crew's colours, dust-capped, with one tiny warm or cyan lamp each.
- They move slowly and softly, never in a rush. Their sounds are tiny, in the pentatonic, heard through the ground.
- **07 reacts:** the first meeting of each species, it stops its head on them and chirps **Curious**. A flock
  tailing 07 gets a glance back and a soft **Happy**.

## 2. Lunar phenomena (the weather)
Real effects, made beautiful. Each one rewards being there, and none of them punishes being elsewhere.

| Phenomenon | What you see | Where and how often | Readability | Reward for being there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Levitating dust glow** (real horizon glow) | a soft glowing haze along the horizon at the terminator, motes drifting up in thin columns | the mare's terminator band; one "glow hour" per ~2 sessions | the horizon brightens, the radio crackles softly, and 07 looks toward it | while it glows, **charged dust clings to buried metal**, so half-buried salvage shows as faint outlines nearby: a salvage-spotting window |
| **Micrometeorite flash** (real impact flashes) | a brief white flash on the horizon, then a small fresh crater with **hot, glowing impact glass** | open regions; about one per 20 min of driving there | the flash, a soft distant "tick" through the ground, and 07 turns its head; the flock (if near) drifts toward it | the fresh crater holds **impact glass**: a bonus of the region's signature material, plus a rare chance of a later-tier one. It glows for ~10 min, and **it stays collectable forever** (the glow is the treat, not a timer). |
| **Gentle moonquake** | a low rumble, dust sliding off slopes, a slow settle | ambient tiny ones are rare; **one scripted per region** at a fixed spot | rumble 0.1 (gamepad), a deep soft sound, a dust slide you can watch | **reveals:** a slope slides to uncover a buried wreck section, a cave mouth or a relic pocket. Never damage, never blocks a way back. |
| **Frost sublimation** | frost on a crater's edge sparkles, then lifts as a shimmering veil when the sun's edge touches it | the Shadowed Crater's rim (0.8), scripted per visit | glitter, then a veil; a crystalline sound | exposes **ice-rich ground** for drilling at the rim for a while (it stays drillable after) |
| **Earthrise** | Earth climbing over a ridge, the whole frame warm-blue | anywhere with a low horizon; about once per 3 sessions, never during a task | the camera's stillness beat offers it (VISION pillar 6) | a databank page and one line of Ro's, a calm photo moment |

**Rule:** phenomena never interrupt a verb, never move 07, and never take control. Any input continues play.

## 3. Rare real life
Very rare. The emotional payoffs.

- **The tardigrade capsule** (0.6, the mare's secret):
  - a small crashed experiment capsule half-buried beyond the swirl's far end, a nod to the real Beresheet lander
    (2019), which carried tardigrades to the Moon;
  - scanning it: "Dormant tardigrades. They curl up and wait. They might still be alive."
  - 07 can tow the capsule home to the lab's keepsake shelf. Its slot shows a tiny lit window.
  - 07 chirps **Curious**, then a long soft **Happy**.
- **The crew's moss** (0.8, the lava tube):
  - in the crew's storm-shelter station, one grow lamp is still lit, and under it Theo's moss is alive, green on
    grey basalt;
  - scanning it opens Theo's page (read by Ro), and the radio plays nothing for a moment;
  - **Moss** (the friend) recognises it later.
  - The game's one moment of real green before the biodome.

## 4. The discovery databank (Subnautica's PDA, improved)
- **Where:** a third tab in Tab, **Notes** (vi: *Ghi chép*), beside Hold and 07.
- **What opens a page:** scanning a species, a phenomenon (scan the fresh crater, the glow, the quake's slide), a
  place (a site's landmark), or life. The verb is the same E scan (0.5.5 §1.2), and scanning life never removes it
  (rule 6).
- **A page holds:**
  - the thing's name;
  - **the line drawing 07 made while scanning** (the scan's own white lines, captured as the page's picture);
  - **one or two sentences** in Kenji's voice (machines) or Ro's voice (the moon, places and life). These are voices,
    so warmth is allowed (writing-style §1), but they stay plain and short, e.g. Kenji on panel mites: "They keep the
    panels clean. Nobody told them we left."
- **Categories:** Machines · Moon · Places · Life. The count shows per category (found of known), never a percentage.
- **No popups** (the guidance budget): a new page shows only as the pickup note "Note added: Panel mites". The page
  waits in Tab.
- **Improved over Subnautica:**
  - the picture is *your* scan, not a stock image;
  - pages are 1–2 sentences, not essays;
  - the voices are people the player cares about, so the databank doubles as the crew's story.

## 6. Contracts and tasks (0.6)
**Core (Director):**
- `SpeciesId`, `ICreatureField` (a nearest group, its state);
- `CreatureReacted(species, reaction, position)`;
- `PhenomenonStarted/Ended(kind, position)`;
- `DatabankEntryAdded(entryId, category)`, `IDatabank`;
- the scan target kinds extended: Fragment, Species, Phenomenon, Place, Life.

| Domain | Tasks | Size |
|---|---|---|
| gameplay | the species' behaviour loops (small state machines driven by light, stillness, listening and distance, pooled, with a behaviour LOD so far groups sleep); the sweeper repair and its road reveal between sessions; the phenomena scheduler (rates, the impact glass drop, the scripted quake reveal); the databank model and save; scan kinds | L |
| art | 4 species (low-poly, a lamp each), the tardigrade capsule, impact glass and fresh crater decals, the dust-glow haze, the quake dust slide | M |
| world | habitats placed in the mare (arrays, the trench line, flock zones), the scripted quake spot, the capsule spot, the terminator band | S |
| rover | 07's reactions (head stop, Curious, the glance back at the flock), stillness with mites | S |
| audio | each species' tiny in-key voice, the impact "tick", the quake rumble, the glow crackle | M |
| ui | the Notes tab (categories, pages with the scan drawing, counts), the pickup note | M |
| story | databank lines (Kenji: machines; Ro: moon, places, life), en and vi | S |

**Acceptance (0.6):**
- A 20-minute mare drive meets 2–4 encounters, at most one group in view at a time.
- No machine ever blocks, traps or chases 07.
- Every species and phenomenon can be scanned into a page with its own drawing and a 1–2 sentence note.
- Following the flock or the trench leads to something worth it.
- The impact glass from a flash can be collected whenever 07 arrives.
- The capsule can be brought home.
- Zero per-frame GC with every 0.6 species active nearby, and the frame budget holds while streaming.
