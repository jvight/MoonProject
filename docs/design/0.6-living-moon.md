# A living moon (0.6+): machine ecology, phenomena, rare life and the databank

> Author: the game design session. Draft for the Director and the owner.
> The owner (2026-10-13, memory `living-moon`): 07 alone driving, mining, crafting and building is boring. It lacks
> the urge and the fun along the way that Subnautica's ecosystem gives. The owner chose **all four layers**.
>
> Sibling of `0.6-world-regions.md` (regions open outward by adjacency, §2.1 there). It builds on:
> - the 0.5.5 spec: scanning (§1), derelicts (§1.7), the guidance budget (§4);
> - feel-pass §7 (07's chirps);
> - M3-25 (Tilly fetches), ruling 15 (the battery).
>
> **Solitude stays the bar** (pillar 6, memory `feel-priority-solitude`). The moon is still mostly silent. Life is
> met, not swarming.

## 0. Rules for everything alive or moving
1. **Never hostile, never horror.** Nothing chases, harms, steals, blocks or startles. A machine in 07's way steps
   aside. Nothing can trap 07 (ruling 7).
2. **Sparse.** At most **one group** of machines in view. A new species encounter about every 3–5 min of driving in
   an open region, and fewer at home. Phenomena are rarer still.
3. **Readable by behaviour, not text.** Each species has a habitat, a behaviour loop and one reaction to 07: shy,
   curious or indifferent. The player learns it by watching.
4. **Every encounter is worth something:** a databank page, a pointer to a find, a path, a helper. Never only
   decoration, never a chore.
5. **Systemic inputs only:** light (sunlit or shadow, 07's lamp), stillness (07 stopped), listening (07's rest ping),
   and distance. No scripted cutscenes for the ecology.
6. **Scanning life never removes it** (the 0.5.5 D2 vanish applies to blueprint fragments only). Scanning a machine,
   a phenomenon or a place draws its lines into the lens and leaves it as it was.

## 1a. The machine ecology: simulation rules
> The owner: "everything must be EXTREMELY LOGICAL". The ecology's lore follows story v2 §3 (`docs/design/story-v2.md`,
> box/story 0f2ca91d): every machine was built or fixed by the crew for a job and set to "continue" when they left.

### 1a.1 The light model (fixed first, because energy depends on it)
- **Today's look:** a "night" lit by a cool key light called earthlight at 19° elevation, casting long shadows, with
  Earth huge and low. It never moves. **That conflicts with solar power:** earthshine is far too weak to charge a
  panel. So either nothing solar could ever work, or the look is wrong.
- **The fix: Lumen Station sits at a near-polar site.** This is real lunar geography and it explains everything we
  already show:
  - **The Sun skims the horizon.** It is always low (≤ 8° elevation) and circles the sky once per lunar day, so
    shadows are always long (pillar 6's "low sun").
  - **High ground is almost always sunlit** ("peaks of eternal light", real). The crew put their **solar arrays on
    the rim's high shoulders**, and the Peak is one of those peaks.
  - **Some craters never see the Sun** ("permanently shadowed regions", real). That is the Shadowed Crater: its
    darkness and cold are true, not a game rule.
  - **Earth hangs fixed** in the sky (the Moon is tidally locked, real) and low on the horizon, as we draw it. It
    gives a constant cool **earthshine fill** that lights the world but **never charges anything**.
  - **The basin floor** is sunlit when the Sun's bearing crosses a low gap in the rim, and earthlit when the rim hides
    it. **A terminator line slowly sweeps across the floor**, and that is where the **levitating dust glow** (§2)
    appears.
- **In the engine (world):** the key light becomes **the Sun** (warm-white, low, slowly circling), and a second,
  fixed, cool light is **earthshine** from Earth's direction.
  - **One lunar day = 2 hours of play** (proposal): a one-hour session sees the Sun move halfway round, and shadows
    turn slowly and calmly.
  - Places get real sunlit and earthlit phases.
  - The current lavender night look becomes the **earthlit phase**. The sunlit phase adds warm-white rims and gold
    on high ground.
- **Owner decision L1 (final, 2026-10-13): the low, slowly circling Sun at a near-polar site.**
  - The Sun skims the horizon, and shadows are long.
  - High ground is nearly always sunlit, and the Shadowed Crater is permanently shadowed.
  - Earth hangs fixed and low; its earthshine lights but never charges.
  - **1 lunar day = 2 h of play.** Today's lavender look becomes the earthlit phase.
  - **Build (0.6 world tech):**
    - **world:** the Sun rig (a low key light circling once per lunar day), the fixed earthshine fill, sky and fog
      phases blending between sunlit and earthlit, the terminator sweep for the dust glow;
    - **art:** the palette per phase (sunlit: warm-white rims and gold on high ground; earthlit: today's lavender)
      and a phase check on every prefab;
    - **rover:** a shadow and charge readout on 07: the flank lamps and the solar wing show whether 07 is in sun
      (trickling) or shade.
  - **VISION edit** (proposed to the Director): see `docs/design/0.6-world-regions.md` §8.
- **Consequence for 07:** Tilly's solar cell trickle (ruling 15) works **only in sunlight**, never by earthshine.

- **SEEK** goes to the **nearest reachable source of its own kind**, within its home range (below). It never seeks a
  kind it can't use.
- **QUEUE:** one machine per pad. Others wait in arrival order at 5 m in an idle pose. **Machines always yield a pad
  to 07** (they were built to serve rovers). Never fighting, never pushing.
- **CHARGE:** a visible pose and time: it settles, its lamp pulses slowly, and a tiny tone rises.
- **DORMANT:** when no source is reachable (the Sun is gone, the pad unpowered, the grid cut). It folds into a
  sleeping pose, its lamp goes out, and dust slowly caps it. It **wakes when its source returns** (the Sun comes
  round, 07 repairs the pad, the tower or mast comes online).
- **DEAD:** a cell that failed with age. It becomes a derelict, and stays one until 07 repairs it.

### 1a.5 07 inside the ecology
- **07 shares the chargers.** Every pad it uses may have a queue, and machines yield to it.
- **Repairs change the ecology visibly, and the change persists (saved):**
  - repair a mast → the flock perches there, its circle's dormant machines wake;
  - restore the array's pad → the sweeper works and the road clears further each session;
  - fit a cell in the convoy's lead → the convoy drives home (watch it on the way);
  - light the tower → the trencher moves again;
  - build the crater pylon → the ice tappers wake.
- **The next trip shows the consequence:** more lamps, more motion, cleaner panels, a longer trench. The chart's
  pencil notes it.
- **A small kindness:** if 07 stays still in sunlight among panel mites, a few climb onto its solar wing and clean it.
  The trickle is slightly faster while they're there. They climb back down when 07 moves.

### 1a.6 Two machine families (story v3, owner 2026-10-13, memory `story-logic`)
The moon holds machines from **two stations**: Lumen (the crew's) and **a second station** beyond the rim, built by
another agency and also silent. They make up roughly half the machine ecology each.

**How the people addressed their machines:** operationally. Protocols, task queues and maintenance settings; never
heartfelt instructions. Behaviour comes from programs, and character shows only through behaviour (07 and the
friends).

**Two power standards (the logical core):**
| | **Lumen family** | **Second-station family** |
|---|---|---|
| Design language | cream enamel, a worn orange stripe, rounded, wheels | a different agency's look: angular, legs or tracks, its own colour code (art and story v3 decide; it never uses Lumen's cream and orange) |
| Charger | **round induction pads** with an amber rim (the dock, mast pads, array pads) | **hexagonal contact docks** with a plug arm that latches onto the machine |
| Cells | Lumen swappable cells; sealed isotope cells in dark places | their own cell format and voltage |
| On loss of contact | Kenji's setting **"continue"**: keep doing the task queue | the agency's standard **"safe mode"**: finish the current step, park, hold position, minimum power, poll for a command link |

**Sharing chargers (the rules):**
1. **No machine charges at the other family's charger.** The connectors and voltages don't match. A Lumen machine
   never queues at a hex dock, and a second-station machine never parks on a round pad.
2. **07 is Lumen-native.** With the **Charge Adapter** (a 07 part, Back socket's side mount; the 0.6 or 0.7 scope is
   story v3's call), 07 can charge at hex docks too. The tooltip shows the name and key glyph only.
3. **A Power Bridge** (a pad structure, M3-22) built beside a charger converts it for both families. **After a bridge
   is built, machines of the other family start using that charger** (they queue by the same rules, yield to 07).
   This is a visible consequence (§1a.5).
4. **Waking the second station's machines takes contact, not just power.** In safe mode they hold position until a
   command link reaches them. **07 repairs their comms node** (their own mast type), and the machines in its range
   resume their last task queue. That is visible on the next trip: legs moving, docks lit, work going on.
5. **Never conflict:** the families never compete (different chargers) unless bridged, and then they queue. Neither
   family is hostile to the other or to 07.
6. **Every second-station derelict has a reason** in the same ledger. For example: safe mode held so long its cell
   finally ran flat, or its dock's array dusted over (they had no mites).

**Which species is which family** (proposal; story v3 confirms):
- **Lumen:** panel mites, the sweeper, the trencher (Lumen's Link cable), the convoy, Mule, the haul sled, and the
  elders 01–06 (all at rest).
- **Second station:** the survey flock (a different agency's survey swarm that follows any rover, because their
  protocol is "escort surface vehicles"), the 0.7–0.8 cable climbers and ice tappers if story v3 places them there,
  and new species from their station (story v3).
- **The second station itself** is a region-scale site (story v3 places it). Its comms node is the first contact
  point, and its docks are the first hex chargers 07 sees.

**Rule:** phenomena never interrupt a verb, never move 07, and never take control. Any input continues play.

## 3. Rare real life
Very rare. The emotional payoffs.

- **The tardigrade capsule** (0.6, the mare's secret):
  - a small crashed experiment capsule half-buried beyond the swirl's far end, a nod to the real Beresheet lander
    (2019), which carried tardigrades to the Moon;
  - scanning it: "Dormant tardigrades. They curl up and wait. They might still be alive."
  - 07 can tow the capsule home to the lab's keepsake shelf. Its slot shows a tiny lit window.
  - 07 chirps **Curious**, then a long soft **Happy**.
- **The crew's moss** (0.8, the lava tube):
  - in the crew's storm-shelter station, one grow lamp is still lit, and under it Theo's moss is alive, green on
    grey basalt;
  - scanning it opens Theo's page (read by Ro), and the radio plays nothing for a moment;
  - **Moss** (the friend) recognises it later.
  - The game's one moment of real green before the biodome.

## 4. The discovery databank (Subnautica's PDA, improved)
- **Where:** a third tab in Tab, **Notes** (vi: *Ghi chép*), beside Hold and 07.
- **What opens a page:** scanning a species, a phenomenon (scan the fresh crater, the glow, the quake's slide), a
  place (a site's landmark), or life. The verb is the same E scan (0.5.5 §1.2), and scanning life never removes it
  (rule 6).
- **A page holds:**
  - the thing's name;
  - **the line drawing 07 made while scanning** (the scan's own white lines, captured as the page's picture);
  - **one or two sentences** in Kenji's voice (machines) or Ro's voice (the moon, places and life). These are voices,
    so warmth is allowed (writing-style §1), but they stay plain and short, e.g. Kenji on panel mites: "They keep the
    panels clean. Nobody told them we left."
- **Categories:** Machines · Moon · Places · Life. The count shows per category (found of known), never a percentage.
- **No popups** (the guidance budget): a new page shows only as the pickup note "Note added: Panel mites". The page
  waits in Tab.
- **Improved over Subnautica:**
  - the picture is *your* scan, not a stock image;
  - pages are 1–2 sentences, not essays;
  - the voices are people the player cares about, so the databank doubles as the crew's story.
