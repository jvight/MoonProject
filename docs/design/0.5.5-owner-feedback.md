| The fragment vanishes with a pop | reads as a placeholder | **It dissolves into its own drawing** (D2): the object becomes the white lines 07 just drew, and those lines stream into the lens. The vanish *is* the learning, made visible. |
| Blueprints appear in a menu | invisible progress | The **lab's tech board** gets a torn sheet per fragment, and the pieces join into the full sheet when the set completes. Kenji's plotter then draws the clean copy in the bay (M3-19). |

### 1.2 The verb
1. **Notice.** Within 15 m, a scannable fragment shows four small cyan **corner brackets** on the object itself: a
   shape, so it's colour-blind safe. Nothing glows on bare ground.
2. **Scan:** look at the fragment and **hold E**.
   - 07 eases to a stop. Its lid lifts fully (the lens opens), and a soft fan of light sweeps the object from the
     lens.
   - The scan time grows with the object's size: small 1.5 s, medium 2.5 s, large 4 s. **The Focus Lens makes scans
     1.5× faster** (§1.6).
   - Letting go keeps the progress (ruling 4). The drawn part stays drawn.
3. **Done** (the closing beat):
   - The object is fully drawn in white lines.
   - **The dissolve** (D2, ~900 ms in total):
     - the solid surfaces fade out over 300 ms, leaving only the white line drawing standing where the object was;
     - the lines unthread from the far end and stream into 07's lens as a ribbon of light (500 ms, ease-in);
     - the lens flares once (100 ms).
     - Nothing is left behind. A faint dust outline on the ground fades over 2 s, so the spot reads as "learned",
       not "deleted".
   - 07 chirps **Curious** (new data) or **Affirm** (a known blueprint).
   - **A known blueprint:** after the dissolve, 3 Metal arrive in the hold (the bundle's flight into the hatch) and the
     pickup note shows it.
   - The pickup note says "Fragment 2 of 3 · Hover-Jump" (or "Known blueprint · +3 Metal").
4. **The last fragment of a set:** the sheet completes on the lab's board at once. A single **ticker line** says
   "Blueprint complete: Hover-Jump. Build it in the bay." 07 chirps **Proud**.

## 4. Guidance budget
### 4.1 Rules
1. **One guidance element at a time.** The elements are: a prompt chip, a place card, a recipe/station card, the next
   line, and a low-battery hint. A **priority** decides which one shows:

| Priority | Element | Shows when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **prompt chip** (glyph + one verb) | the verb is possible *now* on what 07 faces, and hasn't been learned yet |
| 2 | **station / recipe card** | 07 faces or is parked at a station with something to do |
| 3 | **place card** | the first approach to a place (one line of job) |
| 4 | **low-battery hint** | ≤ 30% and not charging; never at full; once per trip |
| 5 | **next line** | only after ~2 min without progress (teaching steps: 20 s), and only while nothing above shows |

   - **Voices (the ticker: letters, Bell, Ro) are not guidance.** They may show with one guidance element.
   - **The prompt chip is the teaching chip.** It shows only until its verb is learned: the first time the verb
     completes (a cut cracks, a scan finishes, a repair completes), the chip retires for good, saved in the prompt
     ledger. After that the world's own cues (seams, brackets, a lit pad) carry it. The verb's key is listed in the
     equipment screen's tooltips (§3.5, name and glyph only) for anyone who forgets.
   - **This order is canonical** for every session, including story's text audit (§2 there aligns with it).
2. **Never repeat.** If an element says it, no other element says it. If the world shows it (a lit pad, a lamp,
   brackets, glinting seams), no text says it.
3. **Hints only when needed.** Every hint has a condition. The charger's "park here" needs a non-full battery **and**
   07 on or near the pad. At boot with a full battery it never shows.
4. **Showing beats saying:**
   - the station's own lamps carry its state (offline plaque, the port lamp lit when 07 carries what it needs);
   - recipe rows are icons plus numbers;
   - 07's chirps carry mood.

## 5. The cut's charge-up has weight
The owner, after 0.5.4 (memory `cut-charge-weight`):
- the charge is too fast and weightless (0.65 s at tier 0);
- the sound doesn't fit;
- the one-by-one lamps "don't look like technology at all";
- 07 is lopsided (the solar panel and the lamp row sit to one side).

The charge-up is designed here as **one moment**: timing, body, visual, sound and camera together. M3-26 §2 is
updated to match.

### 5.1 Timing: a held trigger builds stages, and the piece decides how many
- **Holding E builds the charge in stages:** 1, 2, 3. The beam fires by itself when the charge reaches **the stage
  the piece needs**:

| Piece (M3-26 §4) | Stages needed |
|---|---|
| small | 1 |
| medium | 2 |
| large plate | 3 |
| hull plate | 3 |

  Short work stays short, and big work earns its build.
- **The brace** (a fixed 0.5 s, every fresh charge) carries the weight: 07 plants itself before anything builds.
  Upgrades shorten the stages, never the brace.
- **A warm emitter:** a re-press within **6 s** of the last firing, at the same site, skips the brace and builds
  stages at ×0.6. Working through a site gets a rhythm, never a wait.
- **Release before the needed stage:** the charge drains back (§5.4), and nothing is lost. Release after firing:
  progress is kept (ruling 4, as now).

| Tier | Stage time | Small (fresh / warm) | Medium | Large and hull |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.45 s | **0.95 / 0.27 s** | 1.40 / 0.54 | 1.85 / 0.81 |
| 1 Focus Lens | 0.36 | 0.86 / 0.22 | 1.22 / 0.43 | 1.58 / 0.65 |
| 2 Cutter Ribs | 0.29 | 0.79 / 0.17 | 1.08 / 0.35 | 1.37 / 0.52 |
| 3 Thermal Cutter | 0.22 | 0.72 / 0.13 | 0.94 / 0.26 | 1.16 / 0.40 |

- **The first cut of the game** (a depot small piece) is 0.95 s of charge, longer than 0.65 and *felt*. The depot's
  other three pieces chain warm.
- **A big hull plate** (fresh, tier 0) takes 1.85 s of building: a moment worth watching, not a wait, because each
  stage *does* something (§5.2–5.4).

### 5.2 The anticipation curve
| Phase | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Brace** | 0–500 ms | 07 settles and locks |
| **Build** | per stage, ease-in within the stage | intensity rises fast at the start of each stage and slows into it |
| **Stage lock** | at the end of each stage | a discrete click; the build steps up a level |
| **Full lock** | at the needed stage | a firmer mechanical lock |
| **Held breath** | 120 ms | everything holds still and the whine plateaus |
| **Ignition** | after the breath | feel-pass §3 (flash, recoil, beam) |

The breath is the weight: a beat of stillness before release.

### 5.3 07's body
- **Brace:**
  - 07 lowers **3 cm** on its suspension;
  - the wheels lock with a soft clack, and the rocker arms stiffen (the bogies stop following the ground);
  - the head lowers 3° onto the target, and the lid narrows.
- **Each stage:** a further 0.5 cm settle and a 60 ms micro-tremor in the body. **Never in the camera.**
- **Full lock:** a final settle, then complete stillness through the held breath.
- **Ignition:** the existing recoil (1.5 cm, 2°).
- **Release or drain:** 07 rises back with one soft overshoot (300 ms).

**Symmetry (the owner's point):** 07 is symmetric about its centre plane.
- **The solar wing** moves to the centreline of the back, folding along the spine. It is still *one* tired panel, so
  VISION's "single tired wing" keeps its meaning, now centred.
- **The Twin Solar Wing** (0.7) is that panel unfolding into two halves.
- *VISION text to update (the Director's).*

### 5.4 The visual: requirements any art variant can meet
Art is making 2–3 variants. Each must satisfy:
1. **Symmetric** about 07's centre plane, with **the energy's main path as one centred line along the spine** to the
   emitter (P3). This is a constraint, not the final look: a conduit, a row of segments, a rail or a collar line can
   all satisfy it. Mirrored parts are allowed only as **feeders into** that central path (for example twin capacitor
   drums), never as a left/right pair of lights that reads as two eyes from behind.
2. **Energy travels through hardware:**
   - from a power source (the battery spine or cell module) to the emitter along a **physical conduit** (a cable run,
     a rail, a coil, a glass segment line);
   - never free-floating dots, never "fairy lights" spaced along the body.
3. **Something moves at each stage:**
   - a capacitor ring turns a notch, a vent opens, a collar slides forward, or a coil sleeve extends;
   - light follows the mechanism, not the reverse.
4. **The three stages are readable at 30 m:** three discrete things fill (rings, cells, collars). The needed stage
   shows by how many fill before firing.
5. **One energy colour,** tech-cyan, going toward white at full lock. Emissive only on energy-carrying parts (VISION
   rule).
6. **Drain:** the fill runs backwards to the source in 200 ms. **Warm emitter:** the conduit keeps a faint glow for
   the 6 s window, so the player sees why the next charge is quick.
7. **Tiers** (M3-26 §3) change the hardware visibly: the Focus Lens' ring on the emitter, ribs, a hot core. They
   never add more of the same dots.

Example directions for art (not prescriptive):
- (a) twin mirrored capacitor drums on the back feeding one central conduit up the neck;
- (b) a central spine conduit of three glass segments with sliding sleeves;
- (c) an emitter collar of three rings that extend forward stage by stage.

### 5.5 Sound (replaces the glassy arpeggio)
All of it is heard **through the chassis** (vacuum): low, mechanical and close.
- **Brace:** a short servo "chunk" and the wheel-lock clack.
- **Build:**
  - a **capacitor whine**, a soft sine with a filtered-saw edge, gliding upward;
  - each stage lock is a **detent click** plus the whine stepping onto a pentatonic note: **D → A → D′** for stages
    1–3;
  - a low hum on D underneath, swelling with the stages.
- **Full lock:** a firm two-part "clunk-click", then the whine **holds** its note through the 120 ms breath.
- **Ignition:** feel-pass §3's zing plus thump.
- **Drain:** the whine glides back down to D and fades (200 ms).
- **Warm emitter:** the whine starts already at the D-hum level, with no servo brace.
- Peaks at mezzo-piano, at most three layers (hum, whine, click).

### 5.6 Camera
- **A fresh charge for a large or hull plate:**
  - over the brace and the build, the camera eases **6% closer** and **0.3 m lower**, toward an over-the-shoulder
    frame of the target (ease-in-out, ≤ 1 s);
  - **it holds dead still at full lock** (the breath);
  - after the break it eases back over 1.2 s.
- **Small and medium pieces, and every warm charge:** no camera move. Repeated motion would tire the player.
- **Gentle camera on:** no camera move at all. **Never any shake.**
