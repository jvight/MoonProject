# Opening cutscene: trial script (three variants)

> **Status:** trial script, 2026-10-24. Docs only.
>
> **Author:** the game design session, for the owner via the Director.
>
> **Builds on:**
> - `opening-cutscene-research.md` (§3 references, §4 concepts);
> - story v4 "The Departure";
> - `07-identity.md` (SSR-5, Kessler–Arai 1983, S/N 3-0417, CONTINUE, wake-on-carrier, the call sign three short, one long);
> - `tech-eras.md`;
> - `maker-understanding.md`;
> - `cutscenes.md` §1.8 (the boot face as built);
> - `opening-v3-purpose.md` (the call from the sky point).
>
> **Hard rules:**
> - it ends *into* the existing boot face (CRT power-on → the Kessler–Arai mark → the field profile → the 14-line
>   POST), with no cut in tone;
> - the owner's "no camera tour around 07" (the POST is one shot);
> - no sentimental human → robot moments;
> - plain, few words, native Vietnamese;
> - calm, lonely but peaceful (VISION pillar 6);
> - skippable by any input after 2 s, straight into the boot face;
> - New Game only (Continue keeps the short wake).

**Canon note.** 07 was built in **1983** by Kessler–Arai, and Lumen was commissioned in **1984** (tech-eras). So
07 can't be built *at* Lumen. Variant A shows the maker's line, then the shipment to Lumen.

## 0. Player experience
| | The aim |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | One image the player hasn't seen in a game: a machine left running while the world it served walks away. |
| **Pull** | Questions only, no answers: who were they? Where did the lights in the sky go? Why does the sky now call with *their* signal? |
| **Hold** | Every question is answered *in play*: the ruins, the airlock, the databank, the call. The cutscene plants questions; it doesn't spend them. |
| **Finish** | It bookends the ending: the same pattern of light that calls 07 at minute 1 is the Arks' answer at the end. |
| **Feeling** | **Patience.** Thirty-six years pass in a minute, and nothing is sad on purpose. |

**Never spoiled:** the ice, the survey's content, the crew's fate (`SUIT 01-04 · OFFLINE` stays a discovery at the
airlock), and the Return.

## 3. Variant C: "Site Cam 2" (thirteen thousand days), about 90 s *(recommended)*
**Idea:** the whole film is **one locked frame from the station's own site camera**, pointed at the lander's lee where
07 parks. Time passes in that single frame until the camera itself dies. Then the game's own camera takes over the
same composition in 2024, and pushes in to 07's face.

**Arc:** a working night → left → time → silence → woken.

**Text:** only the camera's stamp, the same in both languages (`SITE CAM 2 · LUMEN-04` plus date and time digits).
**VO:** none.

| # | Time | Camera | On screen | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8 s | the site-cam frame, locked, wide (1980s video: soft, a little warm, scanlines) | night at Lumen, 1988. The cargo lander's amber beacon. 07 rolls the last crate down the ramp; two suit lamps work nearby. | the cam's faint tape hiss; motor whir; one soft A | `SITE CAM 2 · LUMEN-04 · 28 OCT 1988 · 21:40` |
| 2 | 8–20 s | same frame | 07 parks on its trickle post. A suit lamp comes close; a gloved hand flips the switch on 07's flank, and 07's small screen glows `CONTINUE`. The lamp turns away **without a pause**: it is work. | a switch click; suit-radio clicks in the crew pattern, **three short, one long** | the stamp ticks |
| 3 | 20–32 s | same frame | white sparkles across the image (the storm's particles hitting the sensor). Far above, a slow chain of bright points climbs away from Earth (the Arks leaving). The suit lamps walk off toward the habitat and out of frame. Station lights dim to standby. | hush; the hiss rises; a low D swells, then thins | the stamp ticks |
| 4 | 32–56 s | same frame, time-lapse | the date digits race; the low polar Sun circles the horizon again and again, shadows sweeping; Earth's phases; dust creeps over 07's wheels; frost glints at the post; a meteor flash; **the lander's beacon fails** | one sustained low D; pentatonic notes further and further apart | the year counts up: 1989… 1995… 2003… |
| 5 | 56–62 s | same frame | the feed degrades: colour drains, a roll bar, a freeze → `NO SIGNAL`, then black | the hiss cuts; 2 s of silence | `NO SIGNAL` |
| 6 | 62–78 s | **the game's camera**, the same composition in full R1 colour, 2024 | the same lee, now the ruins: dust banks, the dead beacon, 07 under dust. The eclipse ring over the rim; the point of light by Earth blinks **three short, one long**; 07's antenna lamp stutters. | the carrier tone, the **same pattern** as shot 2's radio clicks, faint, on D | — |
| 7 | 78–90 s | a slow eased push to 07's dark face (the inventory's framing: the existing push-in) | the face fills the frame; **the CRT powers on → the boot face** (mark, field profile, POST) | the tube whine, then the boot sounds as built | — |

- **What the player learns, without a word:**
  - 07 is a work machine, set to continue by people who treated it as a tool.
  - They walked away in a storm, and lights left the sky.
  - A very long time passed.
  - Something in the sky now calls with *the crew's own pattern*.
- **What stays a mystery:**
  - who the people were, and where they went;
  - what the lights leaving Earth were;
  - why the call uses their pattern;
  - what's behind the habitat door.

  These are answered in play at the ruins, the airlock, the databank and the ending.
- **The planted find (a bonus):** **Site Cam 2** stands, dead, in the ruins, framed exactly as in the film. Scanning
  it adds a databank entry, and the player recognises the shot. That is the cutscene paying off in play.

## 5. Recommendation: Variant C, "Site Cam 2" (superseded by §7.2 after the owner's review: make D, with C as the fallback)
- **Show, don't tell** (owner rule; Journey, Outer Wilds, Inside): one frame, no words beyond a camera's stamp, and
  the sky does the talking.
- **Solitude and calm** (pillar 6): a locked shot watching time pass is the purest form of "lonely but peaceful".
  Nothing moves fast, and nothing is sad on purpose.
- **No sentimentality:** the people are lamps, and the hand at the switch is a work gesture (07-identity: people
  treated 07 as a tool).
- **It sets up the hook exactly:** the carrier repeats the crew's own call sign, and that is the question opening v3
  is built on.
- **It reuses what exists** (the lander, the post, 07, the sky and eclipse, Earth, the dust), so it is the cheapest.
  Its continuity into the boot face is natural, because it is the same place in the same frame.
- **References:**
  - **Subnautica** keeps its intro short and physical;
  - **Fallout's** newsreel is B's model, great for satire, but satire isn't our tone;
  - **Death Stranding's** long exposition is what we avoid;
  - **Stray** shows a past life through behaviour, as C's lamps do;
  - **Tunic** and **Journey** wake in an old, quiet place under a sky that means something.
- **When:** after opening v3's play is accepted (owner, `opening-cutscene-later`).

## 6. Storyboard frame prompts for agy (Variant C)
Style for all frames:
- R1 stylized realism, Lunarion palette, a near-polar lunar basin;
- frames 1–5 seen through a **1980s CCTV site camera** (soft focus, warm cast, scanlines, a white stamp bottom-left);
- 3440×1440 composition, locked camera at the same position and lens in every frame.

1. **Night shift, 1988:** a cream-and-coral cargo lander with an amber beacon; a small cream rover (07, CRT face,
   mismatched wheel) rolling a crate down the ramp; two distant suit helmet lamps; stamp `SITE CAM 2 · LUMEN-04 · 28
   OCT 1988 · 21:40`.
2. **CONTINUE:** the same frame; 07 parked on a small trickle post in the lander's lee; a gloved hand at 07's flank
   switch; 07's small screen glowing `CONTINUE`; the suit lamp already turning away.
3. **The storm:** the same frame with white particle sparkles across the image; far above the rim, a thin chain of
   bright points rising away from a hazed Earth; two suit lamps small, walking out of frame; station lights dimmed.
4. **Time-lapse:** the same frame; long low sun shadows sweeping (a motion-blur arc); dust half over 07's wheels; the
   lander's beacon dark; the stamp reading `2003`.

7. **The call:** the same as 6, but 07's antenna tip lamp lit cyan for a moment; the sky point visibly brighter; the

8. **The face:** a close-up of 07's dark CRT face (the inventory framing), a single amber power-on dot at its centre.

## 7. Variant D: "The Long Night" (the Departure as awe), about 135 s *(recommended after the owner's review)*
> The owner saw the Site Cam 2 storyboard and called it "too simple, not WOW enough". D keeps C's intimate frame as
> a **recurring device** between huge shots: small ↔ vast is where the wow comes from.
>
> **Still true to the hard rules:**
> - calm (no explosions, no shake);
> - the people are lamps and work gestures only;
> - no ice, no crew fate, no Return;
> - it ends into the existing boot face;
> - skippable after 2 s; New Game only.

**Arc:** a world at work → everyone leaves → the lights go out → decades → one blink answers → a small machine wakes.

**Music:** one D major pentatonic theme. It grows from a single note to **one big swell at the Departure (about
0:58)**, falls to near-silence by 1:25, holds a lone D through the decades, and ends on silence plus one carrier
pattern before the tube's whine.

**Text:** only the Site Cam stamp in shots 4 and 7, plus nothing else. **VO:** none.

**What makes the wow beats land:**
1. **Scale contrast is the engine.** Every huge shot keeps 07 or the lander in frame as a tiny point (Interstellar's
   docking, WALL-E).
2. **One swell only.** Music saved for the Departure and spent once makes it unforgettable. Everything after it is
   quieter (Journey).
3. **Motion in stillness:** slow lights over a dead-still Moon. Vacuum clarity (hard edges, no haze) is our signature
   look.
4. **The network as a character:** it pulses alive, sweeps dark, then one pulse returns at the call.
5. **The intimate frame** (Site Cam 2) between vast shots gives the eye a place to rest, and pays off in the ruins.

**What the player learns:**
- a station full of life;
- everyone leaving, in lights across the sky;
- the lights going out;
- a very long time;
- a call in the crew's own pattern, answered.

**What stays a mystery:** who they were, why they left, where the river went, why the crew never came back out (the
airlock), and what 07's queue is for.

**Canon cautions:**
- **The launches are stylised:** real launches seen from the Moon would be invisible. They read as drive-flares in a
  long exposure. Keep them soft and slow.
- **Earth's lights in 1987 are sparse** (the Long Dim began around 1978–83), and dark in 2024 (story v4 Decision 3).
- **Intact Lumen is shown only from 200 m and above:** no interiors, no crew faces, so the ruins keep their stories.
