**Branch:** `box/agy-feelaudit` (`main` @ `f48d3793`)

## 1. Purpose & Scope

Following the `0.5.6-1ad3cf5b` playtest, the project owner established a hard quality gate before any further feature work:

> *"We have to truly make all of 07's controls, the environment, the atmosphere when you first enter the game, the effects and the environment interaction really good before we can go further."*

This audit pairs a deterministic, input-driven **PlayMode Feel-Capture Harness** (`FeelCaptureRecorder`, `FeelCaptureRoverTests`, `FeelCaptureGameplayTests`, and `tools/feel_capture.py`) with a line-by-line inspection of the runtime codebase and visual inspection of all 8 scenario capture strips and frame sequences in `Logs/audit/`.

### In-Flight Sibling Jobs (Noted, Not Re-Audited in Depth)

Per task rules, the following items are already owned by active sibling branches and are excluded from the fix slices below so work does not collide:
- **Title layout & music:** `agy-titlefix`, `agy-titlemusic` (wordmark/button layout and title theme playback).
- **Opening camera:** `agy-opening` (opening wake camera framing and initial transition).
- **Face inventory tilt, input & blur:** `agy-faceinvfix`, `agy-facecrisp` (`Tab` cargo screen camera tilt, CRT render target resolution, and pointer raycast mapping).
- **Scan aim & VFX:** `agy-scanv2` (scan cone targeting and pulse shader polish).
- **Headlamp aim:** `agy-lampaim` (headlamp pitch/yaw aim alignment).

## 4. Deep-Dive Evaluation Across the 4 Gate Areas

### Area 1: 07's Controls

#### Weight, Acceleration & Braking Curves, Steering, Low-G Float & Slopes
- **Flat Acceleration & Cruise (`driving`):** 07 reaches `90%` of its `7.39 m/s` cruise speed in `2.57 s` with a pleasant initial squat (`2.8°` nose-up pitch). However, braking (`_brakeDeceleration = 8.0 m/s²`, `timeToStop = 1.30 s`) and lateral grip (`_gripRate = 9.0`) clamp planar slip too aggressively for loose lunar regolith (`CTRL-05`). Releasing the stick mid-turn kills yaw rate in `60 ms` (`_releaseSmoothing = 0.06 s`, `postReleaseYawOvershootDeg = 0.0°`, `midTurnReleaseHeadingContinuationDeg = 6.21°`).
  - **Reference Comparison (*Astroneer* / *Death Stranding*):** In *Astroneer*, the Tractor/Buggy carries rotational momentum for `150–250 ms` after releasing steer and exhibits a gentle `0.5 m` rear-axle drift when braking hard on loose regolith, making the vehicle feel like a physical mass on dust rather than a slot car.
- **Visual Wheel Grounding & Pivot Steering (`CTRL-06`):** In `RoverVisualRig.LateUpdate`, `SampleWheelCompression()` raycasts from `_chassisRoot` *before* `ApplyChassis()` applies pitch, roll, and heave to `_chassisRoot`. Consequently, when 07 leans into a high-speed turn (`driving/frame_008_08_full_speed_turn_lean.png`) or lands (`leap`), the chassis roll pushes the outside tires `5.7–11.1 cm` underground (`roverGroundPenetrationMaxMeters = 0.111 m`) while the suspension arms remain un-compressed. At standstill (`driving/frame_006_06_pivot_turn_in_place.png`), turning in place angles the front wheels `24°` Ackermann while spinning all 4 wheels like a tank skid-steer.
- **Slopes (`CTRL-04`):** Climb speed hardly varies between `11.5°` (`7.03 m/s`), `18.2°` (`6.92 m/s`), and `30.5°` (`7.02 m/s`). While `GroundModel` prevents unwanted parked creep (`0.006 m`), `SlideAcceleration` is `0` for all slopes `<= 32°`, meaning a `30.5°` side-slope traverse (`slopes/frame_006_06_side_slope_traverse.png`) has only `1.2 cm` of downhill drift over `2.6 s`. Worse, above `32°`, `SlideAcceleration` is added *after* `Vector3.MoveTowards(desiredVelocity)`, so releasing the throttle downhill accelerates faster than holding throttle.
- **Hover-Jump & Landing (`CTRL-02`, `CTRL-03`):** Charging a jump dips the chassis in two disjoint steps (`ChassisJelly` `-12 cm` at `t = 0`, then `HoverJump` `-8 cm` at `t = 0.15 s` = `20 cm` > `18 cm` suspension travel). Liftoff uses `_riseGravity = 2.6 m/s²` and `_fallGravity = 3.6 m/s²` (not lunar `1.62 m/s²`), reaching `7.30 m` apex (`4.60 s` air time). Then, `8.0 m` above the ground (`_cushionProbe = 8f`), `HoverJump` brakes vertical speed to `-0.54 m/s` (`leap/frame_005_05_cushioned_descent.png`), robbing the jump of a free-fall arc and landing at `1.67 m/s` — just below `AudioDirector.LandingImpactMinSpeed = 1.8f` — while `_tuning.Motion.LandSquash` is never read in `RoverFeel.cs`.
  - **Reference Comparison (*A Short Hike* / *Subnautica* Prawn Suit):** *Subnautica*'s Prawn Suit lets gravity accelerate the descent cleanly and fires retro-thrusters only in the final `1.5–2 m` if the player feathers thrust, punctuated by a heavy mechanical knee-squash and hull thud on touchdown.
- **Collisions (`CTRL-01`):** Driving into a boulder (`collisions/frame_002_02_boulder_cruise_pinned.png`) or scraping a `SEC-04` ruin wall (`collisions/frame_007_07_wall_scrape_after_2_5s.png`) pins 07 at `0.165 m/s` because the hull `BoxCollider` has no low-friction `PhysicsMaterial` and `RoverController` overwrites `rb.linearVelocity` every `FixedUpdate` without projecting `_planarVelocity` along collision contact planes.

#### Camera (`CTRL-08`, `CTRL-09`)
- **Free Orbit Jerk:** `_freeLookDampingShare = 0.15f` (`RoverCameraRig.cs:441`) strips `85%` of position damping during mouse/pad look, producing `11,310 m/s³` camera jerk over terrain ripples.
- **Wall Push-In Clipping:** Against a ruin wall (`camera/frame_005_05_camera_against_wall.png`), the camera compresses to `1.72 m` (`_minCollisionDistance = 1.2 m`) directly into the back of 07's CRT head without lifting the look-at pivot or dithering the chassis.
- **Downhill Framing & Recenter:** Stopping on a downhill slope drops camera pitch from `6.57°` to `4.51°` (`camera/frame_007` vs `frame_008`) because `ComputeSlopePitchOffset` multiplies by `speed01`. Auto-recenter waits `3.5 s` and then sweeps at `90°/s` even when creeping slowly.

#### Verb Inputs, Hints, Rebinding & Gamepad Parity
- **Hold vs Tap & Overlap (`ENV-01`, `ENV-02`):** Scan (`Hold E`) and Salvage Cut (`Hold E`) share the `Interact` button via `GameplaySystem` priority (`Dock > Salvage > Scan > Pickup`), which works when Build Mode is closed. However, when Build Mode (`B`) is open, `BuildField` does **not** consume `Interact`, `Drive`, or `Winch` (`Scroll`), so pressing `E` to place a structure also triggers Salvage/Scan/Dock, and tapping `E` to place immediately cancels welding on the next frame (`ENV-01`).
- **Input Hints (`ATMO-04`):** Context prompts adapt dynamically between Keyboard/Mouse (`[E]`, `[RMB]`, `[Tab]`) and Gamepad (`(A)`, `(LT)`, `(Y)`) via `GlyphLabels`, as verified in `verbs/frame_006` (`LT Lift`) vs `verbs/frame_007` (`RMB Lift`). However, `CrateNote` overlaps `ContextPrompt` in the same top-center slot.
- **Rebinding & Gamepad Parity:** Keyboard and Gamepad bindings are both supported in `PauseMenu` and `RoverInputReader`, and gamepad orbit (`105.3°` in `camera`) and triggers (`LT` Tether, `RT` Excavate) work cleanly in `FeelCapture`, except that continuous driving haptic rumble is missing (`CTRL-07`).

#### Editor vs. Standalone Player Build Risks
1. **Runtime `Shader.Find` Stripping Risk (HIGH):** `FeelMaterials.cs:46, 86` and `RoverPrefabBuilder.cs:717, 742` call `Shader.Find("Universal Render Pipeline/Unlit")`, `Shader.Find("Universal Render Pipeline/Particles/Unlit")`, and `Shader.Find("Universal Render Pipeline/Lit")` at runtime. If those shaders are not referenced in `GraphicsSettings.alwaysIncludedShaders` or a build scene material, standalone player builds strip them and render magenta/invisible VFX.
2. **Frame-Rate Dependent Exponential Decay (MEDIUM):** `SalvageField.cs:620` decays cut progress on release via `progress *= 1f - 0.12f` per `Update()` frame without multiplying by `Time.deltaTime`. At `60 fps` it decays in `0.3 s`; at `240–300 fps` in a standalone build it vanishes almost instantaneously (`0.06 s`).
3. **Synchronous Scene Reload Stall on New Game (MEDIUM):** `GameBootstrap.cs:256` calls synchronous `SceneManager.LoadScene(active.buildIndex)` at `WakeBeat.PushedIn` when a save exists (`ATMO-01`), causing a visible main-thread freeze (`14.7 fps` min in `first_minutes`) that is even longer when loading disk bundles in a standalone player build.
4. **Per-Frame GC Allocation (`906–1137 B/frame`) (LOW-MEDIUM):** All 8 scenarios show a steady baseline allocation of `~588–1137 B/frame` (primarily UI Toolkit layout/visual-tree polling and string/delegate churn), which will trigger incremental GC collection spikes every few seconds on lower-spec target hardware.

- **Wheel Dust, Landing Puffs & `DustSwirl` (`VFX-02`):**
  - Ruling R11 in `docs/VISION.md` explicitly mandates ballistic, vacuum-logical dust with **no billowing clouds**. Currently, `RoverPrefabBuilder.cs:654-700` sets `limitVelocityOverLifetime.drag = 3.0f` (wheel dust) and `2.2f` (landing puff) with `sizeOverLifetime` expanding `2x` (`0.55 → 1.15`) and `gravityModifier = 0.3f` (`0.48 m/s²`), creating billowing terrestrial smoke clouds behind 07 (`camera/frame_007_07_downhill_framing_driving.png`, `slopes/frame_005_05_over_crest.png`).
  - Worse, every jump launch and landing spawns `DustSwirl` (`FeelVfx.cs:108-110`), a ring of 16 procedural spheres with an additive shader at `_Intensity = 2.2` (`FeelMaterials.cs:67, 95-96`) that floats upward in zero gravity (`leap/frame_003_03_liftoff_stretch.png`), looking like a magic spell ring rather than lunar regolith ejecta.
- **Tire Tracks (`VFX-06`):** `RoverWheelFx` only emits tracks from the 2 rear wheels, and `_trackLifetime = 24f` erases them after 24 seconds (`driving/frame_008_08_full_speed_turn_lean.png`). In *Journey* and *Death Stranding*, the player's path carved across the sand/snow is a core emotional reward of traversal.
- **Vacuum Audio Honesty (`VFX-03`):** `JumpAudio.cs:64, 122` plays an `"AirWind"` rushing wind loop during every jump in a vacuum.
- **Cutter & Structure Welding (`VFX-01`, `VFX-05`):** On standard `SalvageField` wrecks (`verbs/frame_003_salvage_cutter_beam_sparks.png`), the 3-layer cutter beam + point light + spark shower reads well at chase distance. On `SealedContainers` and `CrewCrates` (`verbs/frame_005_sealed_crate_cut_missing_beam.png`), the beam is `59.7 m` away at `(0,0,0)` (`VFX-01`). On `BuildField` structure welding (`verbs/frame_010_build_welding_pad.png`), `RepairBeam` is a single thin `0.035 m` line with no weld light and almost no sparks (`VFX-05`).
- **Night, Dawn & Global Sun Extinguish Bug (`VFX-04`, `VFX-07`):**
  - Because `SkyCycleSystem.SampleSunlit` raycasts from 07's position to the sun and feeds `WorldAtmosphere.Apply` (`dayFactor = SmoothStep(0.5f, 1f, sunlit)`), driving into the shadow of a local ridge (`slopes/frame_006_06_side_slope_traverse.png`) or watching dawn below `sunlit = 0.5` (`night_dawn/frame_004_04_dawn_diamond_ring_breakout.png`) turns off the directional `KeyLight` across the **entire lunar basin and distant mountains**!
  - At night (`night_dawn/frame_000_00_night_parked_headlamp_motes.png`), `HeadlampMotes` float in zero gravity in front of a parked rover, and `RearVentStrip` (`0.85` HDR < `1.25` bloom threshold) fails to bloom (`night_dawn/frame_002_02_night_turn_dust_and_rear_vent.png`).
