# Maker understanding: scanning teaches 07 how each maker builds

> Owner-approved pillar (2026-10-19). Author: the story & flow session. Everything below is `[PROPOSAL]` unless it
> cites a decision. Bound by `tech-eras.md` §6 (makers), `databank.md`, `blueprint-fit.md`, `craft-catalogue.md`,
> `07-identity.md` and VISION ruling 11 (visible progression).

## 0. Player experience
- **Hook:** the first elder scanned lights `KESSLER–ARAI · 1/2` on the CRT. One scan later, 07 bolts that elder's
  old white headlamp onto its own cream visor. The scan *became* something on 07.
- **Pull:** four meters, four doors. Tycho sits at `1/3` from the basin's one foreign wreck: a family 07 can't use
  yet, glimpsed early.
- **Hold:** 07 and its base turn into a visible **collage of eras**: an SSR-3 lamp, VIS crate shells, a 1971-style
  reflector, a teal hex plate. Each piece is a souvenir of where the player has been, with no text needed.
- **Finish:** understanding Tycho bridges the two machine families (blueprint-fit's mid-game turn). The great dish's
  final stage needs all four makers at level 2 or more: everything 07 learned goes into the answer.
- **Feeling:** *quiet ingenuity*, making do with what others left.
- **Learn, then improve:** Subnautica's fragments teach one object per hunt, and the hunt turns into a checklist.
  Understanding rewards **breadth of curiosity** (every new thing counts), and its output is 07's own home-made
  version, not a copy.
- **Logic:** 07's scan is a parts profiler (07-identity §1). From enough samples of one maker, it infers that
  maker's fastener standards, connector pinouts and protocols, and its assembly library can then *adapt* the maker's
  parts onto 07's own frames.
- **Calm, no grind:** see the rules in §1.

## 1. Rules
1. **Points:** each **databank entry** a scan completes gives **+1** to its maker. A scan set counts when it is
   complete. Seeded entries count 0, and re-scans or known blueprints count 0. Points are **derived from the
   databank**, so there is no new save state and nothing can desync.
2. **Alongside fragments:** fragments teach **one object**; understanding teaches **a family**. An adapted blueprint
   is never also a fragment blueprint. Where the catalogue already lists the item, its source moves to understanding
   (§3).
3. **Adapted look** (`tech-eras` era N): the donor part **keeps its own era and livery**. 07's bright weld beads and
   a cut `07` tag join it to the frame, and nothing is repainted. That is how the player reads "home-made".
4. **Visible:** every unlock changes 07 or the base on sight (ruling 11).
5. **No grind:** thresholds are small. A region's **on-path** entries reach each due level by the region's end, and
   optional finds only get there sooner. Every maker's supply runs **≥ 30% above** its thresholds, and nothing can be
   farmed.
6. **The opening is protected:** an unlock during B0–B11 is held, and shows after `SignalWest`.
7. **Speed matches the story:** Kessler–Arai is fastest (07 is theirs) and Tycho slowest (foreign).

## 3. Fit with the catalogue and blueprint-fit
- **Standards:** adapted Kessler–Arai items are **B**, VIS and ILC items are **A**, and Tycho's adapter is the **key
  to C** (blueprint-fit F1), so Tycho 2 and up need it. The F2–F6 checks apply unchanged: welds within the work band,
  ≤ 1 cell to build, a robot-base job.
- **Re-sourced catalogue entries:** Storage rack #19, Seismic bell kit #9, Power bridge #9, the charge-port Hex
  adapter, and Cutter Ribs. Their old fragment sources become ordinary databank entries that feed the meter.
- **New entries for the catalogue** (+10): SSR-3 headlamp, grip rings, third socket, module storehouse, tall pylon,
  hangar module, reflector beacon, survey stakes, remote harvester, flock relay drones.
- **New scan-line case** (blueprint-fit §2): `ADAPTED · BUILDABLE` / `TỰ CHẾ · DỰNG ĐƯỢC`.

## 4. UI touchpoints
- **CRT event line** (the scan result, one element): `TYCHO · 2/3` for 3 s when an entry adds a point. The first
  point per maker reads `KESSLER–ARAI · STANDARDS LOGGED · 1/2`. A level-up reads `VIS · ADAPTED · CRATE RACK`.
- **Databank maker cards** (Archive) gain an **understanding meter**: pips up to the next threshold, the adapted
  items already unlocked as lit icons, and the next one as an **outline only**, with no name. Points from before the
  card was found still count.
- **Build menu:** adapted blueprints carry the maker's small mark and the tag `ADAPTED`.
- **Nothing on the HUD.**

## 5. Contracts
- **Core (Director):**
  - reuse the databank's maker enum as `MakerId`;
  - add `UnderstandingGained(MakerId maker, int points, int nextThreshold)`;
  - level-ups use the existing blueprint path (`BlueprintCompleted("adapted.<id>")`), so the build menu needs no
    change.
- **Gameplay:**
  - `MakerUnderstandingTuning` (per maker: thresholds and blueprint ids, `[Tooltip]` and `[Min]`);
  - `MakerUnderstanding`, a plain C# class that derives points from `IDatabank`, applies level-ups idempotently on
    load, and holds announcements until `SignalWest`;
  - EditMode tests: thresholds rise; every id exists in the build catalogue; supply ≥ 1.3 × threshold per phase,
    from a per-phase supply table;
  - two new small scans: the trickle post and the `07 · SPARE` crate.
- **UI:** the maker-card meter, the event-line variants, and the build-menu tag.
- **Rover:** the mounts on 07 (headlamp, grip rings, third socket, hex plate).
- **Art:** the adapted models: donor part + weld beads + `07` tag.
