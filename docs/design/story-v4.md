>
> **Labeling convention:** Facts that already exist in the current codebase or canonical docs are cited with their exact file path, key, or line number. Every new narrative element, timeline detail, shader/sky parameter change, or text rewrite introduced by Story v4 (or carried over from the `Logs/story-v3.md` / `0.6` drafts) is explicitly marked **`[PROPOSAL]`**.
>
> **Core Tone Rule:** Keep the **1980s retro-future aesthetic** (amber phosphor CRT monitors, magnetic cassettes, physical relays, analog gauges) and the **"humanity had to leave Earth" premise** of *Fallout*, **without any** of *Fallout*'s violence, gore, cynicism, mutants, or wasteland combat (`docs/VISION.md` "The Hook", "Tone & Influences"). The world of *Lunarion* remains gentle, warm, melancholic, and dignified. Every narrative beat obeys strict real-world logic: adult scientists and engineers do not write sentimental diary entries to a utility rover; they communicate through operational logs, maintenance tags, and crew-to-crew messages (`Logs/story-v3.md` §4).

## 1. High-Concept Premise

In the late 20th century, a global ecological collapse (`[PROPOSAL]` — see **Section 2** and **Open Question Q1**) dimmed Earth's skies and broke its agricultural cycles. Unable to sustain billions on the surface while the biosphere recovered, humanity pooled its industrial might into the **Orbital Ark Program** (`[PROPOSAL]`): a fleet of rotating habitat cylinders stationed at the Earth–Moon **L4 and L5 Lagrange points** (`[PROPOSAL]`), built to hold human civilization in trust for a century or two while Earth healed below.

**Lumen Station** — a four-person radio-relay and research outpost in a quiet crater on the Moon's near side (`docs/STORY.md` "Premise", line 8) at a near-polar site (`docs/VISION.md` "The Hook", line 43; `docs/design/0.6-living-moon.md` §1a.1, lines 80, 100, owner decision L1) — served in this `[PROPOSAL]` backstory as a deep-space calibration, ice-survey, and communications waystation supporting the Earth–Moon corridor. In **1988** (`[PROPOSAL]`), during the final months of the **Great Departure** (`[PROPOSAL]`), a severe solar storm (`docs/STORY.md` "Premise", line 10) forced Lumen's four crew members (**Ro Amadi, Theo Lindqvist, Ines Kalani, and Kenji Morrow**, `docs/STORY.md` "The crew", lines 20–45) to evacuate aboard the morning shuttle (`log.ines_2`, `Assets/_Project/Data/Localization/en.json:1158-1160`) toward **Ark IV (*Vanguard*)** (`[PROPOSAL]`).

Decades later (`docs/STORY.md` line 14; 36 years later in **2024**, `[PROPOSAL]`), inside the cargo lander's lee during an Earth eclipse (`docs/design/north-star.md` §4a; `Logs/r1/TASK.md` "Keep"), a small six-wheeled utility rover designated **07** (`docs/VISION.md` "07", line 55) wakes up. Above the crater rim hangs an eclipsed Earth (`docs/design/cutscenes.md` §1.2 O4). In the current build, Earth's night hemisphere renders with faint city lights (`SkyCycleSettings._eclipseCityLightStrength = 0.45f` at `Assets/_Project/Scripts/World/Sky/SkyCycleSettings.cs:189`); Story v4 proposes zeroing those city lights (`[PROPOSAL]` — see **Section 7** and **Open Question Q2**) so the dark disc reads immediately: **nobody is home on Earth anymore.**

Yet 07's boot readout reports `EARTH SIGNAL · DETECTED` / `UPLINK · OFFLINE` / `BEGIN RECOVERY` (`lens.boot.signal`, `lens.boot.uplink`, `lens.boot.recovery` in `Assets/_Project/Data/Localization/en.json:1626-1636` and `Assets/_Project/Scripts/World/Lens/LensView.cs:31`). 07 has no speech, no hands (`docs/VISION.md` ruling 14, line 155), and no human consciousness — only its operational directive (`LUMEN RECOVERY PROTOCOL`, `ui.recovery.title` in `Assets/_Project/Data/Localization/en.json:254-256`) and a gentle curiosity (`docs/VISION.md` "07", lines 55–83).

## 2. World History & The 1980s Retro-Future Timeline (`[PROPOSAL]`)

### Why Technology Froze in the 1980s
- **Existing aesthetic facts:** 07 has an amber phosphor CRT face (`Shader "MoonProject/R1/CrtFace"` with `_PhosphorColor` at `Assets/_Project/Shaders/R1/R1CrtFace.shader:1,7`; `docs/art/r1-pipeline.md:67`), the station uses warm amber lamp/beacon tones (`#FFB547` Warm lamp, `#FF8A5B` Warm accent, `#F4E6C8` Cream in `docs/VISION.md` "Palette", lines 205–207), Ro records onto magnetic cassette tapes (`docs/STORY.md` "Ro Amadi", lines 20–25; `en.json:558-564, 750-772`), and the station runs on physical relays, cables, and heavy analog machinery (`docs/DESIGN.md` lines 71–86; `Logs/r1/TASK.md`).
- **Logical explanation `[PROPOSAL]`:** Following the 1969 Moon landing, the world poured its scientific and industrial surplus into heavy orbital lift, nuclear-electric propulsion, closed-loop life support, and rugged radiation-hardened analog electronics rather than consumer miniaturization. When the atmospheric crisis was confirmed in **1979**, every semiconductor fab, steel mill, and chemical plant on Earth was placed under the **Global Ark Allocation Accord**. Consumer R&D stopped cold in **1982**. Standard-issue lunar hardware was frozen around proven, radiation-immune **1980–1985 designs**: 8-bit/16-bit microprocessor boards, amber phosphor CRT monitors, magnetic data cassettes, core-memory vaults, physical relay logic, and brushed-enamel chassis that an engineer like Kenji Morrow could repair with a soldering iron and a multimeter.

## 3. Who Is 07, and What Are 01–06?

### 07's Nature (`docs/VISION.md` "07", Ruling 14; `Logs/story-v3.md` §4)
- **What 07 is:** A 2.2 m × 1.6 m × 1.4 m (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:129`) six-wheeled rocker-bogie utility rover built on a 1984 **Lumen Standard Utility Chassis (Series R1)** (`[PROPOSAL]`). It was the seventh thing Kenji Morrow fixed in their first week, which is why it is named `07` (`docs/STORY.md:9`; `log.kenji_1`, `Assets/_Project/Data/Localization/en.json:1142-1144`).
- **Physical capabilities:** Six wheels on rocker-bogies, a foldable dorsal solar wing, an antenna that blinks the station's call sign (`three short, one long`, `log.kenji_1`, `en.json:1142-1144`), a hooded sensor head with an amber CRT face (`Assets/_Project/Shaders/R1/R1CrtFace.shader:1,7`), and a single front-mounted utility beam used to lift, tow, cut, dig, stitch, and nudge (`docs/VISION.md` ruling 14, lines 155–159).
- **Cognitive level:** 07 is **not** a human mind in a metal body, nor a magical sentient toy. It runs a 1985 heuristic maintenance kernel (`LUMEN-OS v3.4`, `[PROPOSAL]`) with adaptive pathfinding, audio-harmonic homing (tuned to D-major/B-minor carrier frequencies so it naturally rolls toward music and beacons; `docs/VISION.md` "Music", line 87; `log.ro_1`, `en.json:746-748`), and hardware self-preservation routines. Its "personality" — drooping its eyelid when low on charge, perking up at a chime, rolling closer to a warm speaker (`docs/VISION.md` "07", lines 79–81) — emerges honestly from its sensor-homing loops and Kenji's hand-tuned servo dampers.
- **How humans treated 07:** Like a beloved shop dog or a trusty pickup truck (`Logs/story-v3.md` §4). Kenji left maintenance notes on crates for the *next human rotation* (`crate.depot.note`, `crate.garage.note` in `en.json:1774-1776, 1786-1788`), Ines logged survey data (`log.ines_1`, `en.json:1146-1148`), Theo tended seedlings (`log.theo_1`, `en.json:1150-1152`), and Ro used the open-mic radio booth as her late-night show (`log.ro_1`, `log.ro_2`, `log.ro_3` in `en.json:746-748, 1154-1156, 1170-1172`).

## 4. What Is the Player Trying to Do, and Why Does It Matter?

### The Immediate Objective (`LUMEN RECOVERY PROTOCOL`)
When 07 boots during the eclipse, its onboard diagnostic executes the station's automated post-storm checklist (`ui.recovery.title`: `"LUMEN RECOVERY PROTOCOL"`, `Assets/_Project/Data/Localization/en.json:254-256`; `docs/design/gdd-v3.md` §8 lines 346–351):
1. **`Power the station`** (`recovery.power.name`, `en.json:258-260`) — Restore the Radio Tower through its three levels (`upgrade.radio_tower.1..3`, `en.json:506-544`) using the **Tower Coil** (`pickup.part_towercoil` at `Supply Depot`, `en.json:130-132, 518-520`), **Transformer** (`pickup.part_transformer` at `Kenji's Garage`, `en.json:134-136, 530-532`), and **Relay spare part** (`pickup.part_relayspare` at `Service cabinet · any relay`, `en.json:138-140, 542-544`), bringing power to the lander, Kenji's Rover Bay, and the crew's lab chart.
2. **`Link the network to the Peak`** (`recovery.link.name`, `en.json:262-264`) — Repair and light the chain of relay masts (`hop.node.relay.0` `"Mound Relay"`, `hop.node.relay.1` `"Shoulder Relay"`, `hop.node.relay.2` `"Canyon Mouth Relay"`, `hop.node.relay.3` `"Terminus Relay"`, `en.json:882-896, 1366-1396`, and on through the mid-regions to the foot of **The Peak**, `docs/DESIGN.md` lines 151–153).
3. **`Repair the great dish`** (`recovery.dish.name`, `en.json:266-268`) — With Atlas's heavy lift, Superconductor (`material.superconductor.job`: `"The great dish, the Star Tracker, the Anchor Line."`, `en.json:1758-1760`), and the Star Tracker (`docs/features/M3-21-two-tier-goals.md:17`), restore the Great Dish on **The Peak** (`map.region.peak`, `en.json:1446-1452`) — plus `[PROPOSAL]` free its storm-jammed elevation drive ("14% jam" / frozen gimbal, `Logs/story-v3.md` §3).
4. **`Answer Earth`** (`recovery.answer.name`, `en.json:270-272`) — Transmit from **The Peak** back along the incoming signal vector (`ticker.recovery.answer`: `"Earth has heard us."`, `en.json:326-328`). *(If Open Question Q3 selects Ark IV as the signal source, `recovery.answer.name` can either remain `"Answer Earth"` representing the Earth–Moon L4/L5 corridor or `[PROPOSAL]` become `"Answer the signal"`.)*

### Why It Matters (The Emotional & Logical Stakes, `[PROPOSAL]`)
- **To 07:** It is fulfilling the operational recovery protocol queued before the evacuation (`ui.recovery.title`, `en.json:254-256`; `log.ro_3`, `log.kenji_3`, `en.json:1170-1172, 1182-1184`), following the warm D-major carrier tone (`docs/VISION.md` line 87) and keeping the station alive.
- **To the Crew / Ark IV (`[PROPOSAL]`):** When the crew evacuated in 1988 with ten minutes to pack (`log.theo_2`, `en.json:1166-1168`), they had to leave behind **Ines's Core 41 hydrology dataset** (`log.ines_1`, `en.json:1146-1148`; `map.site.drill.note`, `en.json:1338-1340` — proving deep sub-surface lunar water reserves, `Logs/story-v3.md` §3), **Theo's last seedling tray by the window** (`log.theo_3`, `en.json:1178-1180`), and **Ro's homemade golden record** (`relic.golden_record`: `"The Lumen Record"`, `en.json:574-580`). Answering the signal delivers the scientific and cultural archive the crew thought was lost in the storm.
