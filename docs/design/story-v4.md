## 1. The Alternate Timeline ("The Departure" / "Cuộc rời đi") `[PROPOSAL]`

### 1.1 Why Technology Froze at a 1980s Retro-Future Level `[PROPOSAL]`
- **Existing aesthetic facts:** 07 has an amber phosphor CRT face (`MoonProject/R1/CrtFace`, `docs/design/gdd-v3.md` §2), the station uses amber terminal readouts (`#FFB347`, `docs/DESIGN.md` "Palette"), Ro records onto magnetic cassette tapes (`docs/STORY.md` "Ro Amadi"), and the station runs on physical relays, toggle switches, and heavy analog machinery (`Logs/TASK.md`).
- **Logical explanation `[PROPOSAL]`:** Following the 1969 Moon landing, the world poured its scientific and industrial surplus into heavy orbital lift, nuclear-electric propulsion, closed-loop life support, and rugged radiation-hardened analog electronics rather than consumer miniaturization. When the atmospheric crisis was confirmed in **1979**, every semiconductor fab, steel mill, and chemical plant on Earth was placed under the **Global Ark Allocation Accord**. Consumer R&D stopped cold in **1982**. Standard-issue lunar hardware was frozen around proven, radiation-immune **1980–1985 designs**: 8-bit/16-bit microprocessor boards, amber P31/P134 phosphor CRT monitors, magnetic data cassettes, core-memory vaults, physical relay logic, and brushed-enamel chassis that a mechanic like Kenji could repair with a soldering iron and a multimeter.

### 1.3 Why the Arks Have Not Returned Yet `[PROPOSAL]`
1. **The Propellant & Water Ratchet:** Getting ten-million-tonne orbital habitats (or their heavy landing modules) down into a gravity well is a **one-way commitment**. The Arks have enough closed-loop water and reaction mass in their L4/L5 halo orbits to survive for a century at strict rationing, **or** enough delta-v to descend and build a permanent surface civilisation **once** — only if massive, accessible local water-ice exists at the landing site to cracked into liquid oxygen/hydrogen fuel and breathable air.
2. **Earth Remains Unlivable:** Earth's stratospheric haze (*The Great Stagnation*) takes 150–200 years to precipitate naturally. Landing on Earth means suffocating in cold, acid-haze twilight with no solar power.
3. **The False "Dry" Ledger:** Because Lumen's dish failed 46 minutes before the 28 October 1988 uplink, the Ark Fleet Council only ever received the preliminary 14 October report (`<0.2% wt H2O`). Without proof of the deep Shackleton–Lumen aquifer, descending to the Moon would strand humanity on dry rock.

### 2.1 The International Programme: Name, Logo, Signage & Typography `[PROPOSAL]`
Instead of Fallout's cynical 1950s American Vault-Tec satire, Lunarion's retro-future is **1970s–1980s International Space Optimism** — inspired by the real Apollo–Soyuz era, early ESA/NASDA/Interkosmos cooperation, 1970s Swiss international graphic design, and industrial cassette-era hardware (`docs/VISION.md` "What Lunarion is"; `Logs/TASK.md` §2).

- **Programme Name `[PROPOSAL]`:**
  - **The International Lunar Commission (ILC)** (*Ủy ban Mặt Trăng Quốc tế*), operating the **ARK WAYSTATION PROGRAMME** (*Chương trình Trạm Trung chuyển Phương舟 / Hạm đội Cứu Sinh*).
  - Lumen's official designation on stencils: **`ILC · SECTOR SOUTH-04 · LUMEN RELAY & SURVEY`** (paired with the second agency on the terraces: **`TYCHO CONSORTIUM · MERIDIAN AUTOMATED PROSPECTING`**; `Logs/story-v3.md` §1).
- **Logo Idea `[PROPOSAL]`:**
  - **Lumen / ILC Logo:** A clean geometric **crescent Moon cradling a stylized orbital ring and a rising three-ribbed ark hull**, rendered in two flat stencil colours: **Warm Enamel Cream (`#F2E8D5`)** and **Signal Amber/Orange (`#E87A2E`)** (`docs/DESIGN.md` "Palette"; `Logs/story-v3.md` §3.1).
  - **Meridian / Tycho Logo:** A sharp **hexagonal survey crosshair inside a concentric radar wave**, stencilled in **Slate White** and **Teal (`#3A9E9E`)** (`Logs/story-v3.md` §3.1).
- **Typography & Stencil System `[PROPOSAL]`:**
  - **Headers & Hull Markings:** Rounded 1970s Eurostile / DIN-style industrial stencils (`LUMEN-04`, `CARGO LANDER · BAY B`, `ARK IV TRANSIT`).

### 2.3 Radio and Cassette Culture `[PROPOSAL]`
- **Why cassettes and shortwave dominate:** In the 1980s Ark allocation economy, solid-state storage was reserved for navigation computers. - **Ro's "Lumen After Dark":** At a four-person waystation watching humanity pack up the Earth below, Ro Amadi's Sunday night broadcast (`docs/STORY.md` "Ro Amadi"; `docs/design/text/title-and-collections.md` `cassette.after_dark_1`) was more than entertainment — it was the warm human porch light for orbital tug crews, L5 shipyard welders, and Lumen's own four scientists.

## 4. The Arks Today & How the Ending Plays `[PROPOSAL]`

### 4.1 Where the Arks Are Today `[PROPOSAL]`
- **Location:** The **Twelve Orbital Arks** (*Ark I* through *Ark XII*, led by flagship **Ark IV "Vanguard"**) reside in stable **Earth–Moon L4 and L5 Trojan halo orbits** (with long-period resource tugs cycling to near-Earth asteroids).
- **Why L4/L5 is logical:**
  1. Moving ten-million-tonne rotating cylinders out to the outer solar system would require impossible propellant reserves and starve them of solar energy; at **Earth–Moon L4/L5** ($384,400\text{ km}$ from both Earth and Moon), they have uninterrupted 100% solar flux for their mirrors and agricultural rings, zero atmospheric drag, and zero station-keeping fuel cost.
  2. From the Moon's south pole, the L4/L5 clusters sit $60^\circ$ offset from Earth in the sky — well within the beamwidth of Lumen's high-gain Peak dish.
  3. **Why they haven't landed in 36 years:** After two generations in rotating cylinders, the Arks' closed-loop elastomer seals, bearings, and structural hulls are ageing. They *need* a world with gravity, raw regolith shielding, and vast water-ice within the next decade — yet without Lumen's lost 1988 proof, the Fleet Council believes the Moon is bone-dry and is debating a desperate, low-survival burn toward the asteroid belt or Ceres.

### 4.2 Why They Listen to the Moon & What Signal They Wait For `[PROPOSAL]`
- **The Automated Watch & The Sunday Signal:**
  - Before the storm on 28 October 1988, Ines and Dr. Lena Moreau filed an emergency notice (`PRELIMINARY HYDRO-ALERT #88-104`) with the **Ark IV Survey Watch**, stating that full core and radar proof would be transmitted on Lumen's primary carrier (`142.800 MHz`) at `22:00 UTC`.
  - When SPE-88 cut the transmission at `14%`, Dr. Moreau (who evacuated from Geneva to **Ark IV**) and Ro's former orbital comms colleague **Kofi Mensah** petitioned the Fleet Council to keep an **automated Sunday listening window** open on `142.800 MHz` — the exact time slot of Ro's old *"Lumen After Dark"* broadcast — pitching a low-power interrogation ping down at the south pole once a week.
  - It was one of those weekly L5 interrogation pings in **2024** that `relay.2` and Elder **06** caught, waking **07** (`Logs/story-v3.md` §1).
- **What Signal They Wait For:**
  - An automated handshake is not enough to move a fleet. The Ark Survey Watch computer is waiting for a **cryptographically checksummed ILC Hydrological Survey Packet** containing both **physical drill-core assays** (Lumen) and **3D basin radar tomography** (Meridian) proving `>100,000,000 tonnes` of extractable water-ice, paired with a functioning surface landing beacon.

### 4.3 How the Ending Plays (The Dish, The Ice Data, The Answer) `[PROPOSAL]`
1. **Aligning the Dish:** On the Peak, 07 repairs the azimuth drive, clears the storm latch, and slots the **6/6 Survey Archive**. The red fault beacon shifts to a steady **warm amber** (`docs/design/cutscenes.md` §9; `Logs/story-v3.md` §3.2).

The great dish slews slowly past the dark, clouded sphere of Earth and locks onto a faint, steady amber star $60^\circ$ along the lunar orbit: **L5 · ARK FLEET CLUSTER**. 3. **The Beat of Silence:** For five full seconds (`docs/design/cutscenes.md` §9 F4; `Logs/story-v3.md` §0.5), only the dish's cooling ticks and faint static play.
