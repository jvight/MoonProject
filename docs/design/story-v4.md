> **Core Tone & Logic Rule:** Takes *Fallout*'s **1980s retro-future aesthetic** (amber phosphor CRT monitors, magnetic cassettes, physical relays, analog gauges) and its **"humanity is gone from Earth" premise**, **without any** of *Fallout*'s grimness, satire, gore, or violence (`Logs/r1/TASK.md`; `docs/VISION.md` "What Lunarion is", pillars 1–6; "Anti-goals"). *Lunarion* stays cozy, calm, lonely-but-peaceful, and warm. Everything obeys strict real-world logic: humans address machines operationally (protocols, task queues, maintenance notes) and never confide sentimentally in a robot; letters and dispatches are strictly human-to-human (`Logs/story-v3.md` §4; `docs/design/writing-style.md`).

## 1. Locked Story v4 Decisions (`[DECIDED]`)

Following the Round 1 & Round 2 reviews, the five core design decisions are now locked (`Logs/TASK.md` "Decisions"), plus Decision 6 on 07 itself (owner, 2026-10-18):

1. **Decision 1 — The Cause of the Departure (`[DECIDED]`):**
   - **The "Long Dim" / Aerosol Winter (*Mùa Đông Khói Mù / Thập Kỷ Mờ Sương*):** Over about a decade in the late 1970s and 1980s, a persistent global stratospheric aerosol veil blocked sunlight and caused multi-year monsoon and crop failure across both hemispheres.
   - **Strict science & tone rule:** There is no war, no gore, no monsters, and **no claim about surface oxygen declining** (`Logs/TASK.md` Decision 1). Humanity faced a decade-long agricultural famine under dim, pale skies, giving nations a clear, urgent ten-year window to pool industrial capacity into the Orbital Arks and evacuate Earth cleanly.
2. **Decision 2 — How 07 Learns the Crew Died (`[DECIDED]`):**
   - **Sealed Habitat + External Airlock Suit-Status Panel:** During the October 1988 solar storm, the four crew members stayed at their posts in pressure suits to save the water-ice proof and seal the station before retreating to the primary habitat. The primary habitat is sealed and collapsed, and the external airlock panel shows the last suit status: **`SUIT 01-04 · OFFLINE`** with a date (`[PROPOSAL]` `29 OCT 1988 · 04:12`).
   - **Zero corpses:** No bodies or skeletons are ever shown (`docs/VISION.md` "Anti-goals", line 222; `Logs/TASK.md` Decision 2). Their tools and logs lie at the stations where they worked.
3. **Decision 3 — Earth's Night Side & Sky (`[DECIDED]`):**
   - **Fully Dark Continents:** Earth's night side shows **zero city lights** (`SkyCycleSettings._eclipseCityLightStrength` set from `0.45f` to `0.0f` at `Assets/_Project/Scripts/World/Sky/SkyCycleSettings.cs:189`). Only the natural twilight rim, the copper eclipse ring (`SkyCycleSettings._eclipseRingStrength = 1.15f` at `SkyCycleSettings.cs:176`), and a faint polar aurora glow on Earth (a World job is implementing this now; `Logs/TASK.md` Decision 3).
   - **The Ending's Light:** The single artificial point of light that blinks in answer at the ending belongs to **the Arks**, not Earth's surface (`Logs/TASK.md` Decision 3).
4. **Decision 4 — The Arks (`[DECIDED]`):**
   - **Halo Orbit at Earth–Moon L4/L5:** The Orbital Arks wait in a stable halo orbit at the Earth–Moon **L4/L5** Lagrange points. In the lunar sky, they appear as **a faint point near Earth** (`Logs/TASK.md` Decision 4).
5. **Decision 5 — Who Answers (`[DECIDED]`):**
   - **Dr. Lena Moreau Aboard Ark IV:** She is Ines Kalani's radio colleague from 1988 who coordinated automated Meridian's deep-radar telemetry remotely (she was never stationed at Meridian; Meridian is 100% automated and uncrewed). After evacuating to **Ark IV**, she kept Ro's **Sunday listening schedule** open for **36 years**, and she is the one who answers 07's transmission in 2024 (`Logs/TASK.md` Decision 5).
6. **Decision 6 — Who 07 Is and Why It Seems to Feel (`[DECIDED]`, owner 2026-10-18, `docs/design/07-identity.md`):**
   - **Identity:** an ILC **SSR-5 Station Service Rover**, S/N 3-0417, built in 1983 by Kessler–Arai Field Systems; Lumen's modular service rover, whose every verb is a job tool (scan = parts profiler for the shop, cut = salvage torch, build = on-site module assembly). The CRT is a crew diagnostics display; the chirps are a five-tone status annunciator (D acknowledge, E working, F# query, A done, low B fault), already in the game as 07's mechanical voice gestures. It survived 36 years in CONTINUE standby with a wake-on-carrier trigger.
   - **Why it seems to feel:** its **adaptive task scheduler** learned priority weights from outcomes and was never reset in 36 years alone. Everything that reads as character is a weight acting: hesitation on unknown ground, the head drifting to new things, lingering at base at dusk, and a fleet-handshake query tone (F#) twice at an elder with no answer. The weights re-adapt as the player restores power, network and kit, so 07 grows firmer with progress. It never refuses input, never shows emotion words, never mourns.

## 2. The Alternate Timeline (1969–2024)

### 2.1 Why Technology Froze at a 1980s Retro-Future Level (`[DECIDED]`)
- **Existing aesthetic facts:** 07 has an amber phosphor CRT face (`Shader "MoonProject/R1/CrtFace"` with `_PhosphorColor` at `Assets/_Project/Shaders/R1/R1CrtFace.shader:1,7`; `docs/art/r1-pipeline.md:67`), the UI and station lamps use warm amber phosphor tones (`#FFB547` Warm lamp, `#FF8A5B` Warm accent, `#F4E6C8` Cream in `docs/VISION.md` "Palette", lines 196–212), Ro records onto magnetic cassette tapes (`docs/STORY.md:20-25, 63-70`; `en.json:750-772`), and the station runs on physical relays, cables, and heavy analog machinery (`docs/DESIGN.md:71-86`; `Logs/r1/TASK.md`).
- **Historical grounding:** Following the 1969 Moon landing, humanity expanded directly into permanent lunar infrastructure (`Logs/r1/TASK.md`). When the **Long Dim** (`[DECIDED]`) began in the late 1970s, every semiconductor fab, steel mill, and chemical plant on Earth was redirected to build the **Orbital Arks** and their lunar waystations (`Logs/r1/TASK.md` §1). Consumer miniaturization stopped cold in the early 1980s. Standard-issue lunar hardware remained frozen at rugged, radiation-hardened **1980–1985 designs**: 8-bit/16-bit microprocessor boards, amber phosphor CRT monitors, compact magnetic cassettes, physical relay logic, and brushed-enamel chassis that a mechanic like Kenji Morrow could repair with a soldering iron.

### 2.3 Why the Arks Have Not Returned in 36 Years
1. **A One-Way Gravity Commitment:** Descending from the L4/L5 halo orbit into the Moon's gravity well with heavy habitat modules consumes the fleet's reserve propellant. Without verified proof of millions of tonnes of local water-ice (for breathable air, agriculture, and hydrogen fuel), landing on the Moon would strand humanity on dry rock (`Logs/r1/TASK.md` §1).
2. **Earth's Long Dim Takes a Century to Clear:** While Earth's stratosphere slowly precipitates its aerosol veil below, surface sunlight and monsoons remain broken (`[DECIDED]` Decision 1).
3. **The Missing Packet:** Because the 28 October 1988 storm cut Lumen's transmission before completion, the Ark Council only ever had the preliminary 14 October "dry" report—until 07 repairs the Great Dish on The Peak and sends Ines and Meridian's complete survey home (`Logs/story-v3.md` §1).
