## 1. Revised Rules for Swarms & Vacuum Flight (`[PROPOSAL]`)

### 1.1 Revising the Old "Sparse, Met Not Swarming" Rule (`0.6-living-moon.md:13, 39`)
In `docs/design/0.6-living-moon.md:13, 39`, early design held that *"Life is met, not swarming."* Owner feedback (`2026-10-16`, `Logs/TASK.md:18-25`) revised this: **ordinary ground robots alone feel monotonous; flying/hopping collector swarms and big horizon surprises are wanted.** To add swarms without turning the Moon into noisy wallpaper, we replace the old rule with **The Event-Swarm Rule**:

1. **Swarms Are Regional or Phase Events, Never Constant Noise:**
   - **Hard Simultaneous Cap:** At most **1 active swarm (`12–80` units)** and **1 ground machine group** may be active within 07's visual bubble (`250 m`) at any moment.
   - **Time & Trigger Gating:** Each swarm is tied to a **physical lunar phase, place anomaly, or player invention**:
     - **Dust Larks** ride the **dawn/dusk terminator band** (`~10 min` per 2-hour lunar day, `0.6-living-moon.md:104` L1) plus a `60 s` roosting beat at restored Roost Towers.
     - **Lantern Moths** emerge **only in the earthlit night phase** or cave dark, drawn strictly to warm light sources (`#FFB547`).
     - **Plume Drifters** stay parked as flat hexagonal frost-wafers on the dark crater rim until an **Ember Shell** (`inventions.md` §2.2) or **Heliostat beam** (`inventions.md` §2.8) creates a rising supersonic ballistic vapour fountain.
     - **Glassweaver Bees** and **Swirl Rays** are strictly anchored to their geological wonder sites (the **Orange-Glass Crater** and the **Lunar Swirl Field**).
2. **Readable Vacuum Flight Physics & Vacuum-Logical Silhouettes ([`docs/design/vacuum-shapes.md`](vacuum-shapes.md)):**
   Because the Moon is a hard vacuum (`10⁻¹² torr`), **no machine may have helicopter rotors, propellers, air sails, parachutes, kites, insect/bird wings, or aerodynamic tail fins**. Every airborne or hopping species uses one of **four visually readable 1980s lunar flight mechanisms**:
   - **M1 — Electrostatic Corona-Ring Dust Surfing (`Dust Larks`):** Wingless spherical capacitor pods encircled by **concentric horizontal hoops of bare copper and gold corona-discharge wire (`±1.8 kV`)** ride the real **$1000\text{ V/m}$ electrostatic terminator dust fountain** (observed by *Surveyor 7* and *Apollo 17*, `0.6-living-moon.md:300`; `inventions.md` §2.10) via Coulomb repulsion in $1/6\,g$.
   - **M2 — Cold-Gas $\text{O}_2$ De Laval Micro-Puffs (`Lantern Moths`, `Puff Swifts`):** Pressurized **Breather $\text{O}_2$** (`inventions.md` §2.1, §2.5) vented through miniature brass De Laval nozzles on **wingless radial-fin lantern buoys** and **finless spherical-cylindrical Apollo-RCS courier pods**, producing crisp white Joule-Thomson condensation puffs.
   - **M3 — Ballistic $1/6\,g$ Hops & Molecular-Fountain Impingement (`Glassweaver Bees`, `Plume Drifters`):** Spring-loaded + cold-gas parabolic hops on **wingless 6-legged kiln-hoppers** (`Glassweaver Bees`), or **rigid hexagonal snow-crystal copper-louver frost wafers** (`Plume Drifters`, zero canopies or cloth petals) stabilized by 3 bottom cold-gas nozzles inside a supersonic ballistic $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ vapour fountain (`inventions.md` §2.2).
   - **M4 — Crustal Magnetic Lorentz Skimming (`Swirl Rays`):** **Wingless open hexagonal brass frames housing 3 concentric superconducting copper coils** surfing `0.4 m` above the intense $300\text{ nT}$ horizontal magnetic anomaly of a **Lunar Swirl** (`Reiner Gamma`).
3. **Preserved Core Invariants (`0.6-living-moon.md` §0 & §1a; `story-v4.md` §0–§1):**
   - **Never hostile, never an obstacle (`VISION.md` Ruling 7):** Swarms part smoothly around 07 (`3 m` boid repulsion sphere). Nothing harms, steals, startles, or blocks.
   - **Scanning (`[E]`) never removes life (`0.6-living-moon.md:46-48`):** Scanning any member of a swarm captures a custom line-drawing and a 1–2 sentence databank note in **Kenji's** or **Ro's** voice (`0.6-living-moon.md:324-341`).
   - **Two Machine Families (`0.6-living-moon.md` §1a.6; `story-v4.md:24-25, 93-94`):** Every new species belongs visibly to either **Lumen (`ILC`, cream `#F4E6C8` + coral-orange `#FF8A5B`, round amber induction pads, Kenji's `"continue"` mode)** or **Automated Meridian (`Tycho Consortium`, clean white + muted teal `#3CA6A2`, hexagonal contact docks, `"safe mode"` until comms/power restored)**.
4. **Global Swarm Performance Budget (`CLAUDE.md:34` Zero GC):**
   - **Zero `GameObject` / `Transform` per boid:** Each swarm is a single `MonoBehaviour` controller owning a pre-allocated native/struct array (`SwarmAgentState[maxCount]`, updated in a tight `for` loop with zero allocations).
   - **1 Draw Call per Swarm:** Rendered via `Graphics.DrawMeshInstancedProcedural` using an R1 material (`docs/art/r1-pipeline.md`) with per-instance `Matrix4x4` + `_EmissionStrength` / louver-or-ring angle packed in a `ComputeBuffer` / `MaterialPropertyBlock` (`RULES.md:43`).
   - **3-Tier LOD:** **LOD0 (`0–40 m`):** `180–380` tris, vertex-shader ring/louver/leg articulation; **LOD1 (`40–120 m`):** `48–80` tris; **LOD2 (`120–300 m`):** `2-tri` emissive sprite/octahedron; **`>300 m`:** simulation sleeps on a 1 Hz coarse timer.

## 3. Detailed Species Specifications (`S1–S7`, `[PROPOSAL]`)

### 3.1 Species S1: Dust Larks (*Sơn Ca Bụi — Vòng Phóng Điện Tĩnh Điện*) — Seed #1 Developed `[PROPOSAL]`
![Dust Lark Murmuration at Dawn](../../Logs/concepts/dust_larks_v2.png)

- **Job & Builder:** Built in **1983** by **Kenji Morrow** and the **ILC (`Lumen family`)** (`story-v4.md:20, 70, 93`). During the construction of the L4/L5 Arks (`1978–1988`), the fleet's compact D–³He fusion torch tugs and cryo-labs needed **Helium-3 ($\text{^3He}$)** and fine titanium-rich ilmenite dust implanted in the top micron of lunar regolith by solar wind (a real 1980s fusion concept). Strip-mining square kilometres of mare surface was destructive and slow; instead, Kenji built flocks of ultralight **wingless electrostatic corona-ring skimmers** set to **`CONTINUE`** (`0.6-living-moon.md:168, 237`).
- **How It Moves (Readable Vacuum Physics — `vacuum-shapes.md` §3.5):** Because lifting wings cannot generate aerodynamic lift in a `10⁻¹² torr` vacuum, Dust Larks have **zero bird or glider wings**. Each unit (`Ø 0.65 m`) consists of a compact spherical `R1_CreamEnamel` capacitor body (`Ø 0.25 m`) with an `R1_OrangeStripe` equator band and warm `R1_LampAmber` eye lens, suspended at the center of **two concentric horizontal circular hoops of bare `R1_Copper` and `R1_GoldFoil` corona-discharge wire** (`Ø 0.65 m` outer ring, `Ø 0.42 m` inner ring) held by four thin white ceramic insulator struts (`dust_larks_v2.png`). Charging their concentric wire hoops to `+1.8 kV` creates a pure radial electrostatic field (`E ∝ 1/r`) that **surfs the electrostatically levitated dust fountain along the day/night terminator** (`0.6-living-moon.md:300`), banking and tilting by differentially biasing the four quadrant segments of the outer copper ring.
- **Swarm Behaviour (Boids Rules, Size & Schedule):**
  - **Count:** `30–80` corona-ring skimmers per flock.
  - **Boids Weights:** Separation `1.4` (`1.8 m` radius), Alignment `1.8`, Cohesion `1.2`, plus a **Ribbon Spline Attractor (`2.0`)** that pulls the flock in a sweeping figure-eight wave `4–18 m` above the moving dawn/dusk terminator line.
  - **Schedule:** Migrates continuously with the low polar Sun's terminator (`1 lunar day = 2 h`, `0.6-living-moon.md:104`). Once every lunar dawn, the murmuration sweeps directly over the Home Basin and Long Mare, silhouetted against the dark, city-less Earth and the faint L4/L5 Ark point (`story-v4.md:50-54`, `[DECIDED]`).
- **Reaction to 07 (Never Hostile):** **Curiously playful.** If 07 drives beneath the murmuration, the lowest 8–10 Larks dip down to surf the electrostatically kicked-up dust plume behind 07's wheels for `15 s`, making 07 look back and chirp **Happy** (`0.6-living-moon.md:69-70`).
- **What It Collects & Where It Unloads:** Their charged copper-and-gold wire hoops attract $\text{^3He}$-saturated nanofines and rare-earth dust out of the horizon glow. When full, the flock spirals down onto a **Roost Tower (*Tháp Chuồng Sơn Ca*)**—a `4.5 m` cream-and-brass mast with three concentric brass induction perch rings and a central funnel hopper (seen in `dust_larks_v2.png`). Docking on the perch rings pulses their hoop voltage to zero, dropping a glittering golden cascade of fines down the funnel while their spherical capacitors recharge.
- **How 07 Interacts & Gameplay Role:**
  - **Follow:** Following a descending flock at dawn leads 07 to an unmapped **Roost Tower** on the Mare.
  - **Restore a Roost (Base Adoption):** If 07 repairs or builds a **Roost Tower** at Home Base (`2 Alloy + 1 Wiring + 1 Optics`), a flock of 40 Larks **adopts the base**. Every dawn they spiral onto the home roost, glowing with warm amber core lamps and depositing **`1 Rare Earths` + `2 Ilmenite`** (`SalvageMaterial.cs:26, 32`) into the roost hopper for advanced kits.
  - **Scan (`[E]`):** Kenji's databank note: *"Corona-ring skimmers for the dawn line. No air for wings up here—just concentric copper hoops riding the static charge where sunlight hits cold dust, combing out helium-3 fines for the Ark tugs."*
- **Sound in D Major Pentatonic:** A shimmering, wind-chime arpeggio (`D5 · F#5 · A5 · B5 · D6`) generated by electrostatic dust ticks across their concentric copper wire hoops, swelling gently as the wave passes overhead.
- **Performance Budget:** `1` instanced draw call (`DrawMeshInstancedProcedural`, `MaxCount = 80`), gyroscopic ring tilt driven in the vertex shader by `instanceID` phase offset; `LOD0 = 210 tris`, `LOD1 = 56 tris`, `LOD2 = 4 tris`; `0 B/frame` GC.

### 3.2 Species S2: Lantern Moths (*Bướm Đèn — Phao Đèn Cánh Tản Nhiệt Trụ*) — Seed #2 Developed `[PROPOSAL]`
![Lantern Moths Around 07 at Night](../../Logs/concepts/lantern_moths_v2.png)

- **Job & Builder:** Built in **1985** by **Kenji Morrow** (`Lumen family`, `R1_CreamEnamel` + `R1_Brass`) as autonomous **portable work-site floodlight buoys** for crew members repairing cables or tending the **Trial Glasshouses** (`inventions.md` §2.3) during the 55-minute earthlit lunar night (`north-star.md:164`). Their optical sensors were programmed with a simple rule: *seek any warm tungsten/amber work lamp (`2700–3200 K`), hover in a 4 m halo around it, and add your own diffuser glow so the human mechanic has shadowless light.* With the crew gone since October 1988 (`story-v4.md:48`, `[DECIDED]`), they gather around the only warm lamps left on the Moon: **07's headlamps, lit relay masts, and restored bases.**
- **How It Moves (Readable Vacuum Physics — `vacuum-shapes.md` §3.6):** Because spread moth wings look like atmospheric insect wings, Lantern Moths are **wingless vertical cylindrical lantern buoys** (`0.38 m` tall, `lantern_moths_v2.png`): a hanging spherical **amber borosilicate lantern globe (`R1_LampAmber`)** in a brass wire cage at the bottom, a rounded `R1_CreamEnamel` sensor cap with twin brass phototube stalks, a **vertical cylindrical crown of 8 rigid radial `R1_Copper` and `R1_GoldFoil` thermal-radiator fins** on top (like a classic vacuum-tube heatsink or Snap-27 RTG casing, radiating lamp heat into space without any spread wing silhouette), and a bottom brass De Laval micro-nozzle that fires tiny, silent **cold-gas $\text{O}_2$ puffs** (`inventions.md` §2.5) every `1.2 s` to bob vertically in $1/6\,g$.
- **Swarm Behaviour (Boids Rules, Size & Schedule):**
  - **Count:** `12–24` lantern buoys per cluster.
  - **Boids Weights:** Soft orbital curl around the brightest warm light source within `60 m` (Tangential Orbit `1.6`, Separation `1.5` at `0.9 m`, Vertical Bob `0.4 Hz` matched to cold-gas puff intervals).
  - **Schedule:** Dormant by day (docked under solar array eaves and Glasshouse mullions to trickle-charge and top up $\text{O}_2$ from closed-loop Breather manifolds); wakes at **First Nightfall** (`north-star.md` Beat 1) and inside the dark **Lava Tube** (`0.8`).
- **Reaction to 07 (Never Hostile):** **Phototactic-affectionate (purely by program).** When 07 turns on its headlamps at night, nearby Lantern Moths drift over to form a warm, bobbing halo around 07. If 07 stops still for `4 s`, **one or two lantern buoys settle gently onto 07's CRT brow visor** (`lantern_moths_v2.png`), making 07's CRT eyes curve into happy crescents (`^ ^`)!
- **What It Collects & Where It Unloads:** By day they absorb solar charge on array rims and electrostatically brush fine dust off lamp lenses; at dawn they return to **Mast Pad Rings** or **Restored Glasshouse Domes** (`inventions.md` §2.3) to sleep.
- **How 07 Interacts & Gameplay Role:**
  - **Living Night Light (`Shadowed Crater & Night Helper`):** Escorting a cluster of Lantern Moths into a dark wreck or crater rim **doubles 07's effective warm illumination radius** for `3 min` before their micro-cells need a mast pad.
  - **Lure to Glasshouses:** In the earthlit night, a distant golden cloud of Lantern Moths hovering over a frosted dome on the terrace edge guides 07 to **Trial Glasshouse Dome 3** (`inventions.md` §2.3).
  - **Scan (`[E]`):** Ro's databank note: *"Kenji built these little radial-fin lantern buoys so we wouldn't work in our own shadows at night. Turn on a warm lamp, and they still puff over to help."*
- **Sound in D Major Pentatonic:** Soft, breathy cold-gas *pff-tink* notes (`F#4 · A4 · D5`) like tiny glass bells muted in felt.
- **Performance Budget:** `1` instanced draw call (`MaxCount = 24`) + **1 shared pooled shadowless point light** centered on the swarm's centroid (never 24 separate Unity lights!); `LOD0 = 240 tris`, `LOD1 = 64 tris`, `LOD2 = 4 tris`.

### 3.3 Species S3: Puff Swifts (*Én Phụt — Khoang Chuyển Phát Phản Lực RCS*) — Seed #3 Audited & Reframed `[PROPOSAL]`
![Puff Swifts in Flight](../../Logs/concepts/puff_swifts_v2.png)

> **Critical Design Note (Consistency with `story-v4.md`, `inventions.md` §3.1 & `vacuum-shapes.md` §3.4):** In `inventions.md` §3.1, *unassisted catapults firing hollow glass cassette balls across bare rock to uncrewed Meridian* were **CUT** because (1) Meridian is 100% uncrewed (`story-v4.md:55`, `[DECIDED]`), (2) unbraked glass shatters at $40\text{ m/s}$ in vacuum, and (3) loose pickables cannot sit on bare regolith (`0.5.5-owner-feedback.md` §2). **Puff Swifts solve the physics problem** because they are **self-guided cold-gas RCS courier pods with active retro-braking** that fly exclusively between **Ines's Field Survey Camps and Lumen's Relay Mast Catcher-Nets**, carrying cushioned **amber sample/telemetry spheres**! Furthermore, per `vacuum-shapes.md` §3.4, all aerodynamic torpedo fuselages and cruciform tail fins have been stripped: they are **finless spherical-cylindrical pressure pods** stabilized purely by **4 orthogonal Apollo-LM-style De Laval RCS thruster quads**.

- **Job & Builder:** Built in **1986** by **Kenji Morrow** and **Ines Kalani** (`Lumen family`, `R1_CreamEnamel` + `R1_OrangeStripe`) to ferry physical **core-sample ampoules and backup magnetic tape reels** from remote drill camps across the Whispering Canyon and Long Mare back to Lumen's lab without driving a rover back and forth.
- **How It Moves (Readable Vacuum Physics — `vacuum-shapes.md` §3.4):** Compact `0.55 m` **finless spherical-cylindrical pressure vessels** (`puff_swifts_v2.png`) with a riveted `R1_CreamEnamel` tank shell, an `R1_OrangeStripe` equator band, a front `R1_LampAmber` optical rangefinder lens, **four orthogonal flared brass De Laval cold-gas $\text{O}_2$ RCS thruster nozzles** mounted on a central brass manifold ring (`inventions.md` §2.5), and three bottom spring-damped magnetic claws clutching an **amber borosilicate core-sample sphere (`R1_DarkGlass` + `R1_LampAmber`)**. Launched upward from a mast ring, they travel on a textbook **$1/6\,g$ parabolic ballistic arc**, firing **three rhythmic white cold-gas puffs** at launch, mid-course RCS attitude trim, and retro-braking into the destination mast's brass catcher hoop (`puff_swifts_v2.png`).
- **Swarm Behaviour (Boids Rules, Size & Schedule):**
  - **Count:** Always travel in **tight trios (`3` pods in echelon/V-formation)** spaced `2.5 m` apart along a shared parabolic trajectory (`Height = 25–45 m`, `Span = 250–400 m`).
  - **Schedule:** One trio launches every `~12–15 min` between an active/semi-active field mast and a receiving catcher mast.

## 7. Design Variants & Technical Contracts (`[PROPOSAL]`)

### 7.1 Three Ecosystem Variants & Our Ranking (`RULES.md` §"Facts and creativity")
1. **Variant A — Hybrid Vacuum-Logical Event Swarms + Restorable Roosts/Hives (RECOMMENDED, Ranked #1):**
   - *Design:* 6 instanced swarms (`S1–S3, S5–S7`) with 100% vacuum-logical silhouettes ([`docs/design/vacuum-shapes.md`](vacuum-shapes.md)) + 1 horizon leviathan (`S4`), strictly gated by L1 light phases, geological anomalies, and player inventions (`Ember Shell + Frost Harp`, `Roost Tower`, `Hive Kiln`).
   - *Why #1:* Keeps solitude as the baseline (`VISION.md` Pillar 6), makes every swarm scientifically logical in hard vacuum without a single parachute, kite, wing, or windmill blade, and ties swarms directly into the resource/crafting loop (`inventions.md`).
2. **Variant B — Free-Roaming Boid Flocks Everywhere (Ranked #2):**
   - *Design:* Flying drones spawn randomly across all regions on a 3-minute timer.
   - *Why Lower:* Turns swarms into background wallpaper, breaks the lonely Mare mood (`0.6-world-regions.md:25`), and disconnects species from habitats.
3. **Variant C — Scripted Set-Piece Cutscene Swarms Only (Ranked #3):**
   - *Design:* Swarms appear only once during fixed camera triggers.
   - *Why Lowest:* Violates `0.6-living-moon.md:45` Rule 5 (*"Systemic inputs only... No scripted cutscenes for the ecology"*) and removes systemic replayability (restoring roosts/hives).
