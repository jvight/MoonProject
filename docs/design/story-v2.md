# Story v2: the pull outward (0.5 → 1.0)

> Author: the story & flow session, for the owner's review before anything is built. It builds on `docs/STORY.md`
> (Lumen Station, the storm, "keep the radio on", Ro answers) and today's additions:

## 1. The arc in one page
**The theme: waiting is a kind of hope.** Everything on this moon kept doing its last job:
- the machines;
- the tardigrades curled in their capsule;
- Theo's moss;
- Ro, writing for twenty years;
- 07, keeping the radio on.

The story is the player finding out that nobody here gave up.

**The central mystery: who woke me, and where did the others go?**
- 07 was **the seventh thing the crew fixed**. Kenji numbered every machine he brought back to life, and 01 to 06 are
  07's elders, small rovers and carts in the crew's colours.
- Before leaving in the storm, Kenji did not shut them down. He set them to "continue". It felt less like goodbye, and
  the crew meant to be back in weeks.
- Years later, a faint signal from Earth started the **Lumen Recovery Protocol**. The station had no crew, so the
  protocol sent the elders out one by one, each on one errand toward the great dish: fetch, relay, climb, light.
- **Each elder stopped at exactly the obstacle 07 must later overcome:** range, slope, dark and cold, a drop, the
  Peak's rifts. The last one, **06**, stayed near home and spent its final charge relaying Earth's signal to the dock.
  **That is what woke 07.**
- The player meets 06 in the basin and sees what it did. From then on, **the chart in the lab shows five faint
  dotted tracks** fanning out from home: the elders' last known headings, each ending in a small pencil "?".
  - Those tracks are the pull: **Subnautica's Aurora, but made of family.**
  - Each region holds one elder at the end of its track, and finding it explains the gate and points at the next.

**The second thread: the silence.**
- The masts' letters tell the crew's side: they landed safely, the program closed, and Ro kept writing.
- At the mare's comms dish (0.6), twenty years of Ro's letters arrive in a rush, and then **they stop.** The archive's
  last date is long ago.
- The player fears Ro stopped. The truth comes slowly: **the dish stopped listening, not Ro.** Elder 05 kept the mare
  dish aimed at Earth until its cell ran out, and the archive ends on the day 05 did.

**The ending.**
- At the Peak, the red beacon that has blinked since the first frame is **01**, the eldest, too worn to move. It has
  kept its lamp on so that whoever came next would know where to go.
- 07 and Atlas raise the dish, the Star Tracker aims it, and Bell sends Side B of Ro's mixtape up the network.
- The beacon turns from red to warm amber: 01 can rest.
- After a long silence, a light on Earth's night side blinks the call sign, three short and one long. The line
  appears: "I heard you, 07. I'm still here. — Ro."
- She never stopped. The station did, and 07 started it again.

**After the ending:**
- Ro's letters keep coming (`docs/design/text/radio-letters.md`).
- The open goal is **bringing the elders home**: each one towed or ferried to a row of berths by the dock, "Kenji's
  seven", lit one by one. They don't wake; they rest where the crew can see them when they come.

## 3. Where the machines come from
**One rule:** every machine was built or fixed by the crew for a job, and set to "continue" when they left. They are
still doing that job, gently and pointlessly, until 07 gives the job a purpose again.

**07's elders (Kenji's first six):**
- **01:** the signal rover that rode up the Peak road with Kenji to install the dish. It is the red beacon.
- **02:** the shelter cart, sent to stock the lava-tube shelter. It is the moss-keeper.
- **03:** Theo's ice cart for the garden. It is frozen at the crater rim.
- **04:** the lift cart that carried parts up the terraces. It slid back to the shore.
- **05:** the dish-tender at the mare. It ran down, and the archive ended with it.
- **06:** the relay-keeper by the canyon mouth hut. It woke 07.

07 itself was the survey rover Kenji kept for "next time" (his second log, the spare wheel).

**The working machines (machine ecology, `0.6-living-moon.md`):**

| The sweeper | kept the solar-field road clear for the crew's rovers | it still sweeps a road no one drives. 07 is the first wheel on it in decades. Repaired, it sweeps for 07. |

## 4. How the story is told
**Never:** cutscene exposition, voice acting, quest logs, popups. **Always:** the world first, words last.

| Channel | Carries | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Objects and poses** | the elders' last positions, headings and what they still hold; the crew's remnants | none: the scene is the sentence |
| **07's reactions** | the first look at each elder (head held, a soft low chirp); a glance back at the flock | wordless chirps in D pentatonic |
| **The chart** (lab) | the elders' five dotted tracks; each "?" becomes a small drawing when found | pencil, no words |
| **Elder logs** (the scan page of an elder) | one machine line: what it was doing and where it stopped | **plain, functional, machine-terse**, e.g. "06 · RELAY · EARTH SIGNAL → 07 · CELL 0%" / "06 · CHUYỂN TIẾP · TÍN HIỆU TRÁI ĐẤT → 07 · PIN 0%". The plainness is what moves. |
| **Notes databank** (Tab) | one or two sentences per machine, place, phenomenon or life | Kenji: machines ("They keep the panels clean. Nobody told them we left."). Ro: the moon and life. Ines: places. Warmth allowed: these are voices. |
| **Ro's letters** (masts, the mare archive) | the human side over twenty years | Ro's voice, warm (the poetry lives here) |
| **Crew logs** | the crew's moments: the storm, the choice, "If anything grows, tell me" | each crew member's voice |
| **Bell** | signals toward finds; rare one-liners | Bell's character (Ro's machine) |
| **The radio** | the weather loop, now-playing, the clearing static of each lit region | the station's broadcast |
| **Functional text** | prompts, cards, hints | plain, native Vietnamese (`writing-style.md`); never carries the story |

## 5. The moments a player remembers
1. **06 by the hut (0.5).**
   - The player scans a slumped little cart and reads one machine line: it relayed Earth's signal to 07, then its cell
     hit zero.
   - 07 holds its head on 06 and chirps once, low.
   - The player realises: *I was woken by someone who used their last power to do it.*
2. **The archive goes quiet (0.6).**
   - Lighting the mare dish pours in twenty years of Ro's letters, dates flicking past.
   - Then nothing. 05 sits under the dish, its panel still tilted at Earth.
   - The player is left with a question they will carry to the end: did she stop?
3. **The moss under the lamp (0.8).**
   - In the shelter the crew never reached, one grow lamp is still on, and green moss grows on grey basalt.
   - 02 sits beside it, awake. It kept the lamp on for decades.
   - Theo's log line arrives: "If anything grows, tell me." 07 can tell him now.
4. **The red light was a friend (1.0).**
   - The beacon the player has watched since minute one is 01, holding its lamp up for whoever came next.
   - When the dish turns, the light goes amber: 01 rests.
5. **She never stopped (the ending).**
   - The call sign blinks back from Earth: "I heard you, 07. I'm still here."
   - The silence of the second moment resolves: it was the station that went quiet, never Ro.
